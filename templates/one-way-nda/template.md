# ONE-WAY NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

> **Drafting note:** This agreement protects only the Company's information and is drafted in the Company's favour. Use it when the Company alone will disclose, for example to a prospective vendor, developer, manufacturer, channel partner or acquirer. If the Recipient will also disclose material information, use the Mutual NDA. Many institutional investors and venture funds decline to sign NDAs for an initial pitch; do not rely on this agreement to protect information shared with investors who have not signed it. Delete all drafting notes before signing.

This One-Way Non-Disclosure Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is made on {{effective_date}} (the **"Effective Date"**)

**BETWEEN**

**{{company_name}}**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 with corporate identity number {{company_cin}}, having its registered office at {{company_address}} (the **"Company"**, which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include its successors and assigns);

**AND**

**{{counterparty_name}}**, {{counterparty_description}}, having its registered office or principal place of business at {{counterparty_address}} (the **"Recipient"**, which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include its successors and permitted assigns).

The Company and the Recipient are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## Recitals

A. The Company intends to disclose certain Confidential Information to the Recipient so that the Recipient may: {{purpose}} (the **"Purpose"**).

B. The Recipient has agreed to receive and hold the Confidential Information on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the Company making Confidential Information available to the Recipient and the mutual promises in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement:

(a) **"Affiliate"** means, in relation to a Party, any entity that directly or indirectly Controls, is Controlled by, or is under common Control with that Party, where **"Control"** means the ownership of more than fifty per cent of the voting share capital of an entity or the right to appoint a majority of its board of directors or equivalent governing body;

(b) **"AI Tool"** means any artificial intelligence or machine-learning model, system, service or software, including any large language model, generative AI service, chatbot, coding assistant, embedding or vector-search service and any automated analytics service that retains, learns from or is trained, fine-tuned or evaluated on inputs, whether operated by the Recipient or by a third party;

(c) **"Applicable Law"** means all laws, statutes, rules, regulations, notifications, directions, orders and judgments of any governmental, regulatory, judicial or quasi-judicial authority in India or elsewhere that apply to a Party or to the subject matter of this Agreement;

(d) **"Authorised Persons"** means those directors, officers and employees of the Recipient, and those professional advisers of the Recipient, who need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose, and any other person whom the Company has approved in writing;

(e) **"Business Day"** means a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday on which banks are open for general business in {{arbitration_seat}};

(f) **"Confidential Information"** means all information, in any form or medium (whether written, oral, visual, electronic, magnetic or otherwise, and whether or not marked or identified as confidential), that is disclosed or made available, directly or indirectly, by or on behalf of the Company or any of its Affiliates to the Recipient or any Authorised Person, or which the Recipient or any Authorised Person otherwise obtains or observes (including by visiting the Company's premises or accessing its systems), before, on or after the Effective Date, including: (i) business plans, strategies, forecasts, budgets, pricing, margins, financial information, fundraising information and capitalisation details; (ii) information about customers, users, suppliers, investors, employees and other business relationships; (iii) products, services, roadmaps, product designs, specifications, prototypes, user interfaces and research and development; (iv) source code, object code, algorithms, models, model weights, prompts, training data, datasets, databases, software architecture, APIs, credentials and technical documentation; (v) inventions (whether or not patentable), know-how, trade secrets, processes, methods, formulae and techniques; (vi) Personal Data; (vii) the existence, status and terms of discussions between the Parties; and (viii) all notes, analyses, compilations, summaries, extracts, AI Tool outputs and other materials prepared by or for the Recipient that contain, reflect or are derived from any of the foregoing;

(g) **"Personal Data"** has the meaning given to "personal data" in the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023;

(h) **"Residuals"** means information retained in the unaided memory of a person; and

(i) **"Trade Secret"** means any Confidential Information that derives independent commercial value from not being generally known and that is the subject of reasonable efforts to maintain its secrecy.

1.2 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

(a) the recitals and schedules form part of this Agreement, and references to this Agreement include them;

(b) headings and the table of contents (if any) are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation;

(c) words in the singular include the plural and vice versa, and words importing a gender include every gender;

(d) a reference to a "person" includes any individual, company, limited liability partnership, partnership firm, trust, society, association, government or governmental authority, in each case whether or not having separate legal personality;

(e) the words "include", "including" and "in particular" are illustrative and do not limit the generality of the preceding words, and the ejusdem generis rule shall not apply;

(f) a reference to a statute or statutory provision includes any amendment, modification, consolidation, re-enactment or replacement of it, and any subordinate legislation made under it, in each case for the time being in force;

(g) a reference to "writing" or "written" includes email but excludes instant messaging and social-media messaging;

(h) a reference to a Clause, sub-clause or Schedule is to a clause, sub-clause or schedule of this Agreement;

(i) where a period is expressed to run from or after a specified day or event, that day or the day of that event is excluded, and if the last day of the period is not a Business Day, the period ends on the next Business Day;

(j) a reference to a Party includes its successors and permitted assigns; and

(k) no provision of this Agreement shall be construed against a Party on the ground that the Party was responsible for its preparation.

## 2. Obligations of the Recipient

2.1 The Recipient shall:

(a) use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose whatsoever, including not for its own benefit or the benefit of any third party;

(b) keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential and not disclose it to any person other than Authorised Persons in accordance with Clause 2.2;

(c) protect the Confidential Information using at least the degree of care it uses to protect its own most sensitive confidential information, and in no event less than a reasonable degree of care, including reasonable security practices and procedures and access controls appropriate to the nature of the information;

(d) not copy, reproduce or record the Confidential Information except to the extent strictly necessary for the Purpose, and ensure that every copy bears the Company's confidentiality notices;

(e) not reverse engineer, decompile, disassemble, test or analyse the composition or structure of any software, prototype, sample or other material forming part of the Confidential Information;

(f) not use the Confidential Information to develop, or assist any third party to develop, any product, service, process or business that competes with that of the Company, or to file any patent, design or other intellectual property application that claims or discloses any Confidential Information;

(g) not use the Confidential Information to approach, solicit or deal with any customer, supplier, investor or employee of the Company; and

(h) keep a record of the Authorised Persons to whom Confidential Information has been disclosed and provide a copy to the Company on request.

2.2 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to an Authorised Person only if the Authorised Person: (a) needs to know it for the Purpose; (b) has been informed of its confidential nature and of the terms of this Agreement; and (c) is bound by written obligations of confidentiality (or, in the case of a professional adviser, by professional duties of confidentiality) no less protective than those in this Agreement. The Recipient shall not disclose Confidential Information to any Affiliate, contractor or sub-contractor without the Company's prior written consent.

2.3 The Recipient is responsible for every act or omission of an Authorised Person or any other person to whom it discloses Confidential Information which, if done by the Recipient, would be a breach of this Agreement, and shall, at the Company's request, take such steps as the Company may reasonably require to enforce the confidentiality obligations of any such person.

2.4 The Recipient shall notify the Company in writing promptly, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours, after becoming aware of any actual or suspected loss of, or unauthorised access to, use of or disclosure of, Confidential Information, shall provide all information reasonably requested by the Company about it, and shall take all steps reasonably required by the Company to mitigate its effects and prevent recurrence.

2.5 Nothing in this Agreement permits the Recipient to use or rely on Residuals. The Recipient acknowledges that information does not cease to be Confidential Information by reason of being retained in the memory of any person.

> **Drafting note:** Recipients (especially large technology companies) sometimes ask for a "residuals" clause allowing them to use information retained in unaided memory. Clause 2.5 rejects this. A residuals clause substantially weakens protection for know-how and should be resisted unless the commercial relationship requires it.

## 3. Artificial Intelligence and Machine-Learning Tools

3.1 The Recipient shall not, and shall ensure that Authorised Persons do not, without the Company's prior written consent:

(a) input, upload, paste, transmit or otherwise make available any Confidential Information to any AI Tool;

(b) use any Confidential Information to train, fine-tune, ground, evaluate, benchmark or otherwise develop or improve any AI Tool or model; or

(c) create embeddings, vector representations or other derived datasets from the Confidential Information.

3.2 Any output of an AI Tool that contains, reflects or is derived from Confidential Information is itself Confidential Information and is the property of the Company.

> **Drafting note:** Clause 3.1 is an outright prohibition, which suits a disclosing party. If the Recipient's ordinary workflows depend on an enterprise AI deployment, the Company may approve specific tools in writing (for example, tools deployed within the Recipient's own environment, or provided under enterprise terms that prohibit retention of inputs and training on them). Record any approval in an email or side letter.

## 4. Exclusions

4.1 The obligations in Clauses 2 and 3 do not apply to information which the Recipient can demonstrate by clear and contemporaneous written records:

(a) is or becomes generally available to the public other than as a result of a breach of this Agreement or any other obligation of confidence owed to the Company;

(b) was lawfully in the possession of the Recipient, free of any obligation of confidence, before it was disclosed by or on behalf of the Company;

(c) is lawfully received by the Recipient from a third party who is not under any obligation of confidence (direct or indirect) to the Company in respect of it; or

(d) is independently developed by or for the Recipient by persons who have had no access to the Confidential Information.

4.2 Information is not within any exclusion merely because individual features or elements of it are in the public domain or in the Recipient's possession, unless the particular combination and its principle of operation are also so, or because it is embraced by more general information that falls within an exclusion. The burden of proving that any exclusion applies is on the Recipient.

## 5. Compelled Disclosure

5.1 The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent required by Applicable Law or by any court, tribunal or governmental or regulatory authority of competent jurisdiction, provided that, to the extent legally permitted, the Recipient:

(a) gives the Company prompt prior written notice of the requirement, the information to be disclosed and the authority requiring it, so that the Company may seek a protective order or other remedy;

(b) cooperates with the Company, at the Company's reasonable cost, in any effort to resist or narrow the disclosure or obtain confidential treatment;

(c) takes into account the Company's reasonable requests as to the timing, content and manner of disclosure; and

(d) discloses only that portion of the Confidential Information which, on the advice of its legal counsel, it is legally required to disclose.

5.2 Information disclosed under this Clause 5 remains Confidential Information for all other purposes.

## 6. Personal Data

6.1 To the extent the Confidential Information includes Personal Data, the Recipient shall: (a) process that Personal Data only for the Purpose and only in accordance with the Company's written instructions; (b) implement reasonable security safeguards to prevent a personal data breach; (c) not transfer that Personal Data outside India, or to any third party, without the Company's prior written consent; (d) notify the Company without undue delay, and in any event within twenty-four (24) hours, of any personal data breach affecting it, and provide all information and assistance the Company reasonably requires to notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected Data Principals; and (e) erase that Personal Data on completion of the Purpose or earlier on the Company's request.

> **Drafting note:** The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 commence in phases; check which obligations are in force on the date of signing. Where the Recipient will process Personal Data on the Company's behalf beyond a short evaluation, also sign a Data Processing Agreement. Restrictions on cross-border transfer under Applicable Law may be relevant if the Recipient is outside India.

## 7. Return and Destruction

7.1 On the Company's written request at any time, and in any event on termination or expiry of this Agreement, the Recipient shall within five (5) Business Days, and shall procure that each Authorised Person shall: (a) cease all use of the Confidential Information; (b) at the Company's option, return to the Company or securely destroy or permanently erase all Confidential Information in its possession or control, including all copies, notes, analyses and AI Tool outputs derived from it; and (c) deliver to the Company a certificate signed by an authorised officer of the Recipient confirming compliance with this Clause.

7.2 Clause 7.1 does not require the Recipient to destroy or erase: (a) Confidential Information that it is required to retain by Applicable Law, provided the Recipient notifies the Company of what has been retained and why; or (b) electronic copies held in automatic back-up or archival systems in the ordinary course that are not accessible to personnel other than information technology staff, provided that such copies are not restored or accessed except as required by Applicable Law and are overwritten or deleted in the ordinary course of the Recipient's back-up cycle.

7.3 Any Confidential Information retained under Clause 7.2 remains subject to this Agreement for so long as it is retained, notwithstanding the expiry of any period in Clause 10.

## 8. Ownership, No Licence and No Obligation

8.1 All Confidential Information is and remains the exclusive property of the Company (or its Affiliates or licensors, as applicable). Nothing in this Agreement grants the Recipient any licence, ownership interest or other right in or to any Confidential Information or any patent, copyright, design, trade mark, Trade Secret or other intellectual property of the Company, other than the limited, revocable, non-transferable right to use Confidential Information for the Purpose in accordance with this Agreement.

8.2 If the Recipient provides suggestions, comments or other feedback regarding the Company's Confidential Information, products or services (**"Feedback"**), the Company may use and exploit the Feedback without restriction or payment, and the Recipient hereby assigns to the Company, for the full term of protection and throughout the world, all rights, title and interest (including copyright) in the Feedback.

8.3 Confidential Information is provided "as is". The Company makes no representation or warranty as to its accuracy or completeness and shall have no liability arising from the Recipient's use of it, except as may be set out in a definitive written agreement between the Parties.

8.4 Neither this Agreement nor any disclosure under it obliges the Company to disclose any particular information or to enter into any further agreement or transaction with the Recipient. The Company may end discussions at any time for any reason.

## 9. Non-Solicitation and Non-Circumvention

9.1 During the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months after its termination or expiry, the Recipient shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment or engagement any employee or consultant of the Company with whom it had contact, or of whom it became aware, in connection with the Purpose, other than through general advertisements not targeted at the Company's personnel.

9.2 During the term of this Agreement, the Recipient shall not use any Confidential Information to approach, or enter into any transaction with, any customer, supplier, investor or business contact of the Company introduced to it or identified to it by the Company in connection with the Purpose, so as to circumvent the Company.

> **Drafting note:** Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 makes agreements in restraint of a lawful trade, profession or business void, subject to a narrow sale-of-goodwill exception. Clause 9.2 is therefore limited to the term and tied to misuse of Confidential Information. Indian courts have reached differing views on post-term non-solicitation covenants; the employee non-solicit in Clause 9.1 is kept narrow and time-limited, but its enforceability after termination is not assured. Rely primarily on the confidentiality obligations.

## 10. Term and Survival

10.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until the earlier of: (a) the expiry of {{disclosure_period}} from the Effective Date; and (b) the date on which the Company terminates it by written notice to the Recipient (which the Company may do at any time).

10.2 Notwithstanding termination or expiry of this Agreement, the Recipient's obligations in respect of Confidential Information shall continue for a period of {{confidentiality_period}} from the date of termination or expiry, except that: (a) in respect of any Trade Secret, source code or Personal Data, those obligations shall continue for so long as the information remains a Trade Secret or confidential, or for so long as the Recipient holds that Personal Data, as applicable; and (b) the obligations in Clause 7 continue until fully performed.

10.3 Clauses 1, 3.2, 4 to 8, 9 (for the periods stated in it), 10.2, 10.3 and 11 to 15 survive termination or expiry of this Agreement. Termination or expiry does not affect any rights or remedies accrued before it.

## 11. Representations and Undertakings of the Recipient

11.1 The Recipient represents and warrants to the Company, as at the Effective Date, that: (a) it is duly incorporated or constituted and validly existing under the laws of its place of incorporation or constitution (or, if an individual, is of the age of majority and competent to contract); (b) it has full power and authority, and has taken all action necessary, to enter into and perform this Agreement; (c) the person signing this Agreement on its behalf is duly authorised to do so; and (d) entering into and performing this Agreement will not breach any agreement or obligation binding on it.

11.2 The Recipient undertakes that it has in place, and shall maintain for so long as it holds any Confidential Information, reasonable technical and organisational measures (including access controls, encryption of Confidential Information at rest and in transit where reasonably practicable, and logging of access) to protect the Confidential Information against unauthorised access, use, disclosure, alteration or loss.

11.3 On reasonable prior written notice, the Recipient shall answer the Company's reasonable written questions regarding its compliance with this Agreement and, if the Company reasonably suspects a breach, permit the Company or its nominated independent auditor to verify such compliance, subject to reasonable confidentiality undertakings in respect of the Recipient's own information.

## 12. Remedies and Indemnity

12.1 The Recipient acknowledges that the Confidential Information is of a special and unique character, that damages alone would not be an adequate remedy for any actual or threatened breach of this Agreement, and that the Company would suffer irreparable harm from any such breach.

12.2 Accordingly, and without prejudice to any other right or remedy available to it, the Company shall be entitled to: (a) specific performance of the Recipient's obligations under this Agreement in accordance with the Specific Relief Act, 1963; (b) a temporary or perpetual injunction restraining any actual or threatened breach of this Agreement, including under sections 37, 38 and 42 of the Specific Relief Act, 1963 and Order XXXIX of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908; and (c) interim measures from a court under section 9, or from the arbitral tribunal under section 17, of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, in each case without first having to exhaust the procedure in Clause 13.2 and without proof of actual damage.

12.3 The Recipient shall indemnify and keep indemnified the Company, its Affiliates and their respective directors, officers and employees (each an **"Indemnified Person"**) from and against all losses, damages, liabilities, penalties, costs (including reasonable legal fees) and expenses suffered or incurred by any Indemnified Person arising out of or in connection with: (a) any breach of this Agreement by the Recipient or any Authorised Person or other person to whom the Recipient has disclosed Confidential Information; and (b) any claim by a third party, or any proceeding by a regulator (including the Data Protection Board of India), arising from such a breach.

12.4 The Company shall notify the Recipient promptly of any third-party claim for which it seeks indemnity under Clause 12.3(b), and shall consult the Recipient in good faith on the conduct of that claim; provided that the Company shall retain conduct of any claim that relates to its Confidential Information, intellectual property or regulatory standing.

12.5 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes the Recipient's liability for breach of this Agreement, and the Company's rights under Clause 12.3 are in addition to its right to compensation under section 73 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.

> **Drafting note:** Since the Specific Relief (Amendment) Act, 2018 took effect, specific performance is the general rule rather than a discretionary remedy, subject to the exceptions in the Act (including sections 11(2), 14 and 16). Note that section 14 bars specific performance of a contract that is in its nature determinable; because the Company may terminate this Agreement on notice, the more reliable route to restrain misuse of Confidential Information is a prohibitory injunction enforcing the negative covenants (sections 38 and 42 of the Specific Relief Act, 1963) and interim relief under section 9 of the Arbitration Act, which is why both are preserved expressly [verify the application of section 14 to surviving confidentiality covenants]. This Agreement deliberately contains no liquidated damages sum: under section 74 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, a stipulated sum is recoverable only as reasonable compensation not exceeding that sum, and the loss from a confidentiality breach is hard to pre-estimate. Never describe any amount as a penalty.

> **Drafting note:** The indemnity in Clause 12.3 is recognised under sections 124 and 125 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872, and Indian courts have held that an indemnity holder may enforce an indemnity once its liability has become absolute, without first paying the loss [verify current authority before relying on it in a dispute]. Recipients frequently ask to limit the indemnity to direct losses arising from third-party claims, to add a cap, or to delete it. A reasonable fallback is to keep Clause 12.3(b) (third-party and regulatory claims) and rely on section 73 damages for direct loss. If a cap is conceded, carve out wilful breach, fraud, Trade Secrets, source code and Personal Data, or apply a separate higher cap to them.

## 13. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

13.1 **Governing law.** This Agreement, and any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or in connection with it, its subject matter or formation, or its breach, termination or validity (including non-contractual disputes or claims), shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of India.

13.2 **Amicable resolution.** If any dispute, controversy or claim arises out of or in connection with this Agreement (a **"Dispute"**), either Party may give the other a written notice describing the Dispute in reasonable detail (a **"Dispute Notice"**). Authorised representatives of the Parties with authority to settle the Dispute shall meet (in person or by video conference) within ten (10) days after service of the Dispute Notice and shall attempt in good faith to resolve the Dispute.

13.3 **Arbitration.** Any Dispute that is not resolved within thirty (30) days after service of the Dispute Notice (or such longer period as the Parties may agree in writing) shall, to the exclusion of the jurisdiction of any court save as provided in Clause 13.5, be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 (the **"Arbitration Act"**), as follows:

(a) the arbitral tribunal shall consist of a sole arbitrator jointly appointed by the Parties within thirty (30) days after either Party proposes, in writing, the name of one or more persons to act as arbitrator; failing such joint appointment, the arbitrator shall be appointed in accordance with section 11 of the Arbitration Act;

(b) no Party shall have the right to appoint the arbitrator unilaterally, or to require the other Party to select the arbitrator from a panel of names chosen by the first Party alone;

(c) the seat, or legal place, of the arbitration shall be {{arbitration_seat}}, India;

(d) the venue of hearings shall be {{arbitration_venue}}, or such other place as the arbitrator may determine after consulting the Parties, and the arbitrator may conduct any hearing wholly or partly by video conference; a change of venue, or the holding of a hearing at any other place, shall not change the seat;

(e) the language of the arbitration shall be English;

(f) the arbitrator shall give a reasoned award, and may award costs (including reasonable legal fees) in accordance with section 31A of the Arbitration Act; and

(g) the award shall be final and binding on the Parties, who undertake to carry it out without delay.

13.4 **Confidentiality of proceedings.** The Parties shall keep the existence of the arbitration, all submissions, evidence and documents produced in it, and the award confidential, save to the extent disclosure is required to enforce or challenge the award, by Applicable Law, or under section 42A of the Arbitration Act.

13.5 **Courts.** Subject to Clause 13.3, the courts at {{arbitration_seat}} shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any application or proceeding under the Arbitration Act in relation to the arbitration (including applications under sections 9, 11, 27, 34 and 36), and over any matter arising out of or in connection with this Agreement that is held not to be arbitrable. Nothing in this Clause 13 prevents either Party from applying to a court of competent jurisdiction for urgent interim or injunctive relief under section 9 of the Arbitration Act, or to the arbitral tribunal under section 17 of the Arbitration Act, at any time, including before or during the procedure in Clause 13.2.

13.6 **Pre-institution mediation.** If any Dispute falls to be decided by a court (rather than in arbitration) and constitutes a commercial dispute of specified value within the meaning of the Commercial Courts Act, 2015, the Parties shall, unless the suit contemplates urgent interim relief, first exhaust pre-institution mediation in accordance with section 12A of that Act and the rules made under it.

13.7 **Continued performance.** Pending resolution of any Dispute, the Parties shall continue to perform their respective obligations under this Agreement, save to the extent that this Agreement has been terminated.

> **Drafting note:** Clause 13.3 provides for ad hoc arbitration with joint appointment. For institutional arbitration, replace sub-clause (a) with the model clause of a recognised institution, such as the Mumbai Centre for International Arbitration (MCIA), the Delhi International Arbitration Centre (DIAC) or the India International Arbitration Centre (IIAC), and check that institution's current rules and fee schedule; institutional appointment avoids a section 11 application if the parties cannot agree. Do not give either party the unilateral right to appoint the arbitrator, or to nominate a panel from which the other must choose: the Supreme Court held unilateral appointment invalid in Perkins Eastman Architects DPC v. HSCC (India) Ltd (2019), and a Constitution Bench confirmed that unilateral appointment clauses offend the equal-treatment principle in Central Organisation for Railway Electrification v. ECI-SPIC-SMO-MCML (JV) (2024). Always name the seat expressly and keep it distinct from the venue, to avoid the seat-versus-venue disputes that followed BGS SGS Soma JV v. NHPC Ltd (2019). For small claims, the parties may agree to the fast-track procedure under section 29B of the Arbitration Act. The specified value for commercial disputes under the Commercial Courts Act, 2015 (currently understood to be three lakh rupees) should be checked at the time of the dispute [verify]. If a party is outside India, consider an institution and seat acceptable to both and check that the award will be enforceable where the counterparty's assets are located (New York Convention reciprocity).

## 14. Stamp Duty and Execution

14.1 **Stamp duty.** This Agreement shall be stamped, on or before its execution, in accordance with the stamp law in force in the State in which it is first executed and, if it is subsequently received in or brought into any other State in India where a higher duty is payable, in accordance with the stamp law of that State. Stamp duty (including any differential duty) and any penalty payable on this Agreement shall be borne by {{stamp_duty_payer}}. If any duty or penalty is payable because a Party brings this Agreement into another State, or produces it before an authority, other than the State of execution, that Party shall bear the incremental amount.
