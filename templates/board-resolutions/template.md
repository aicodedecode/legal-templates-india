# BOARD RESOLUTIONS PACK

> **Drafting note:** This pack contains ten stand-alone resolutions. Use each one as a separate certified true copy (**CTC**) extract of the minutes of the Board meeting at which it was passed. The meeting particulars use the same placeholders in every section; fill them in separately for each meeting. Under section 118 of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Secretarial Standard on Meetings of the Board of Directors (**SS-1**), minutes must be prepared and signed within 30 days of the meeting [verify], and a CTC must reproduce the resolution exactly as recorded in the signed minutes. Every resolution states the type and date of the meeting (or the date of passing by circulation), the quorum, the enabling provision of the Act, and the persons authorised to sign and file. Under section 179(3) of the Act, certain powers can be exercised only by resolutions passed at a meeting of the Board, not by circulation. These include issuing securities, borrowing, approving financial statements and the Board's report, and approving amalgamations. Resolutions 3, 4, 5 and 8 must therefore be passed at a meeting (which may be held through video-conferencing, as the Act permits). Every form name and timeline below should be checked against the current Act, the rules and the MCA portal before filing [verify].

## Part A – Common Heading and Certification

Each resolution in Part B is to be preceded by the following heading, completed as applicable:

**CERTIFIED TRUE COPY OF THE RESOLUTION PASSED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF {{company_name}} (CIN: {{company_cin}}) AT ITS MEETING HELD ON {{meeting_date}} AT {{meeting_time}} AT {{meeting_venue}}**

**Registered office:** {{company_address}}

**Type of meeting:** [OPTION A: Meeting of the Board of Directors held in person / OPTION B: Meeting of the Board of Directors held through video-conferencing or other audio-visual means in accordance with section 173(2) of the Companies Act, 2013 and the rules made under it]

**Quorum:** {{directors_present}} directors were present throughout the meeting, constituting the quorum required under section 174 of the Companies Act, 2013. {{chairperson_name}} chaired the meeting.

Each resolution in Part B is to be followed by the following certification:

Certified to be a true copy of the resolution passed by the Board of Directors of {{company_name}}.

For {{company_name}}

Signature: ____________________

Name: {{certifying_director_name}}

Designation: Director (DIN: {{certifying_director_din}}) / Company Secretary (Membership No.: ____)

Place: {{certification_place}}

Date: {{certification_date}}

> **Drafting note:** The certification is usually signed by a director or the company secretary. Banks and the Registrar of Companies accept CTCs signed with a digital signature certificate, but some banks still insist on a wet-ink signature with the company's rubber stamp. Check the requirements of the receiving institution. A company is not required to have a common seal, and if it has one, its use is governed by the Articles.

## Part B – Resolutions

## 1. Opening of a Bank Account

**Enabling provision:** section 179(1) of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Articles of Association of the Company.

**RESOLVED THAT** the consent of the Board of Directors of the Company be and is hereby accorded to open a {{bank_account_type}} in the name of the Company with {{bank_name}}, {{bank_branch}} (the **"Bank"**), and that the Bank be and is hereby authorised to honour all cheques, payment instructions, bills of exchange, promissory notes and other instruments drawn, accepted or made on behalf of the Company, and to act on any instructions given in relation to the account, provided they are signed or authorised by {{bank_signatories}}, in the manner set out below:

(a) for transactions up to {{bank_single_limit}}, by any one of the authorised signatories singly; and

(b) for transactions above that amount, by any two of the authorised signatories jointly;

whether the account is in credit or overdrawn.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the authorised signatories be and are hereby authorised, in the same manner, to give instructions for net-banking, corporate internet banking and other electronic modes of payment; to appoint users and administrators of the internet banking facility and set their transaction limits; to receive account statements; and to close the account.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Bank's standard account opening form, terms and conditions, and mandate be and are hereby approved, and that {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}, be and is hereby authorised to sign and submit the account opening form and the related documents (including the KYC documents of the Company and its beneficial owners) on behalf of the Company, and to provide the Bank with a certified true copy of this resolution.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** this mandate will remain in force until the Bank receives written notice of its revocation or amendment by a resolution of the Board, certified by a director or the company secretary.

**Authorised to sign and file:** {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}.

> **Drafting note:** No filing with the Registrar of Companies is needed. Banks usually ask for their own format of resolution and for the KYC of all directors, authorised signatories and beneficial owners (under the Prevention of Money-laundering (Maintenance of Records) Rules, 2005 and the RBI KYC Master Direction). For a private placement, the Company must open a separate account used only for monies received under the issue (section 42 of the Act). Make it clear in the resolution that the account is a "Private Placement Account" and that no payments may be made from it except for adjustment against allotment or for refund.

## 2. Appointment of an Additional Director

**Enabling provision:** section 161(1) of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Articles of Association of the Company. Where the appointment is of a nominee director, also section 161(3) of the Act and the shareholders' agreement.

**RESOLVED THAT** pursuant to section 161(1) and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the rules made under it, and the Articles of Association of the Company, {{new_director_name}} (DIN: {{new_director_din}}), who has consented to act as a director in Form DIR-2 and has given a declaration in Form DIR-8 that he/she is not disqualified from being appointed under section 164 of the Act, be and is hereby appointed as an Additional Director of the Company in the category of {{new_director_category}} with effect from {{appointment_date}}, to hold office up to the date of the next annual general meeting of the Company, or the last date on which the annual general meeting should have been held, whichever is earlier.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Board notes the disclosure of interest given by {{new_director_name}} in Form MBP-1 under section 184(1) of the Act.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}, be and is hereby authorised to:

(a) file Form DIR-12 with the Registrar of Companies within 30 days of the appointment;

(b) make the necessary entries in the register of directors and key managerial personnel;

(c) issue a letter of appointment to the director; and

(d) do all acts, deeds and things necessary to give effect to this resolution.

**Authorised to sign and file:** {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}.

> **Drafting note:** An additional director holds office only until the next annual general meeting. Regularise the appointment by an ordinary resolution of the shareholders under section 152 of the Act; this also requires Form DIR-12 [verify]. The Articles must permit the Board to appoint additional directors. The new director needs a DIN (applied for through Form DIR-3, or as part of SPICe+ at incorporation). Check that the appointment does not take the Board above the maximum permitted by the Articles, and that at least one director is resident in India under section 149(3). An investor nominee may instead be appointed as a nominee director under section 161(3), if the Articles provide for it. Board resolutions for appointing directors are not among the section 179(3) resolutions that must be filed in Form MGT-14, so no MGT-14 is needed.

## 3. Allotment of Shares

**Enabling provision:** sections 39, 42 and 62(1)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013; the Companies (Prospectus and Allotment of Securities) Rules, 2014; the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014; the special resolution of the shareholders passed on {{shareholder_resolution_date}}; and, for non-resident allottees, the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019.

**RESOLVED THAT** pursuant to sections 39, 42 and 62(1)(c) and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the rules made under them, the special resolution passed by the shareholders of the Company on {{shareholder_resolution_date}}, the private placement offer cum application letters in Form PAS-4 issued on {{offer_letter_date}}, and the application money of {{subscription_amount}} received in the separate bank account of the Company maintained for this purpose, the Board hereby allots {{securities_description}} to the persons, in the numbers and for the consideration set out below:

| Name of allottee | Residential status | Number and class of securities | Issue price per security | Total consideration received |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| {{allottee_name}} | {{allottee_residential_status}} | {{securities_number}} | {{issue_price}} | {{subscription_amount}} |

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the securities so allotted will rank pari passu with the existing securities of the same class in all respects and will be subject to the memorandum and Articles of Association of the Company, and, in the case of preference shares, to the terms of issue approved by the shareholders.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the securities be credited to the demat accounts of the allottees, and that the Board notes that the allotment is made within 60 days of receipt of the application money.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}, be and is hereby authorised to:

(a) instruct the depository and the registrar and transfer agent to credit the securities;

(b) pay stamp duty on the issue of the securities;

(c) file the return of allotment in Form PAS-3 with the Registrar of Companies within 15 days of allotment;

(d) for each non-resident allottee, file Form FC-GPR through the FIRMS portal of the Reserve Bank of India within 30 days of allotment;

(e) enter the allotment in the register of members and the register of allotments; and

(f) do all acts, deeds and things necessary to give effect to this resolution.

**Authorised to sign and file:** {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}.

> **Drafting note:** This resolution exercises the section 179(3)(c) power to issue securities and must be passed at a meeting. For a private company, Board resolutions under section 179(3) are exempt from filing in Form MGT-14 under the exemption notification of 5 June 2015 [verify]. The special resolution of the shareholders under sections 42 and 62(1)(c) must still be filed in Form MGT-14 within 30 days. Section 42 requires allotment within 60 days of receipt of money. If allotment is not made, the money must be refunded within the next 15 days, failing which interest at 12% per annum is payable from the 60th day. PAS-3 is due within 15 days of allotment for a private placement [verify]. Private companies that are not small companies must issue securities only in dematerialised form, under Rule 9B of the PAS Rules [verify deadlines]. Otherwise, share certificates must be issued within 2 months under section 56(4). Stamp duty on the issue of shares is payable at the uniform rate introduced by the 2020 amendments to the Indian Stamp Act, 1899 [verify].

## 4. Approval of ESOP Plan and Grant of Options

**Part 4A – Approval of the ESOP Plan and recommendation to the shareholders**

**Enabling provision:** section 62(1)(b) of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule 12 of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014.

**RESOLVED THAT** pursuant to section 62(1)(b) and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013, Rule 12 of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, and the Articles of Association of the Company, and subject to the approval of the shareholders by special resolution, the draft {{esop_plan_name}} (the **"Plan"**) placed before the Board be and is hereby approved, with an option pool of {{esop_pool_size}} equity shares of the Company. The Board recommends the Plan to the shareholders for approval by special resolution.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** a general meeting of the shareholders be convened on {{egm_date}} to consider the approval of the Plan, and that the draft notice and explanatory statement under section 102 of the Act, containing the disclosures required by Rule 12(2) of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, be and are hereby approved.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Plan will be administered by the Board [OPTION A: itself / OPTION B: through its Nomination and Remuneration Committee] (the **"Administrator"**), which is authorised to determine the eligible employees, the number of options to be granted, the exercise price, the vesting schedule (subject to a minimum vesting period of one year from the date of grant) and the other terms of each grant, in accordance with the Plan.

**Part 4B – Grant of options (for use after the shareholders have approved the Plan)**

**RESOLVED THAT** pursuant to the Plan approved by the shareholders by special resolution on {{shareholder_resolution_date}}, the Board hereby grants options to the employees named below, on the terms set out against their names and in the grant letters approved by the Board:

| Name of employee | Designation | Number of options | Exercise price per option | Vesting schedule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| {{grantee_name}} | {{grantee_designation}} | {{options_number}} | {{exercise_price}} | {{vesting_schedule}} |

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}, be and is hereby authorised to:

(a) issue grant letters to the grantees;

(b) maintain the register of employee stock options in Form SH-6;

(c) file Form MGT-14 for the special resolution approving the Plan within 30 days of its passing; and

(d) do all acts, deeds and things necessary to give effect to these resolutions.

**Authorised to sign and file:** {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}.

> **Drafting note:** An ESOP requires a special resolution of the shareholders under section 62(1)(b), and that resolution must be filed in Form MGT-14. Under Rule 12, a private company cannot grant options to promoters, the promoter group, or a director who holds (directly or indirectly) more than 10% of the equity. A DPIIT-recognised startup is exempt from this restriction for 10 years from incorporation [verify]. The minimum vesting period is one year. Disclosures in the Board's report are governed by Rule 12(9) [verify]. Grants at an exercise price below fair market value may have tax consequences. ESOP taxation and the deferral for eligible startups should be checked under the Income-tax Act, 2025. If an employee welfare trust is used, additional rules apply. A grant of options is not an issue of securities, but the later allotment on exercise is, so pass resolution 3 (allotment) on exercise and file PAS-3 if required [verify whether PAS-3 is needed for an ESOP allotment by a private company].

## 5. Approval of a Preferential Issue by Private Placement

**Enabling provision:** sections 42, 62(1)(c) and 179(3)(c) of the Companies Act, 2013; Rule 14 of the Companies (Prospectus and Allotment of Securities) Rules, 2014; Rule 13 of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014; and, for non-resident investors, the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019.

**RESOLVED THAT** pursuant to sections 42, 62(1)(c) and 179(3)(c) and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the rules made under them, and subject to the approval of the shareholders by special resolution, the consent of the Board be and is hereby accorded to offer, issue and allot up to {{securities_number}} {{securities_description}} at an issue price of {{issue_price}} per security (including premium), aggregating up to {{subscription_amount}}, by way of private placement on a preferential basis to the identified persons set out in the explanatory statement (the **"Proposed Allottees"**).

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Board notes and accepts the valuation report dated {{valuation_report_date}} issued by {{valuer_name}}, a registered valuer, and, for the non-resident Proposed Allottees, the valuation certificate issued by a chartered accountant or SEBI-registered merchant banker under the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019. The issue price is not lower than the fair value determined in those reports.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the draft private placement offer cum application letter in Form PAS-4 placed before the Board be and is hereby approved. After the special resolution is passed, the letter will be issued to the Proposed Allottees by name, and a record of the private placement offer will be maintained in Form PAS-5.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the Company open a separate bank account to receive the application money for this issue, and that no money be withdrawn from it except for adjustment against allotment or for refund.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** a general meeting of the shareholders be convened on {{egm_date}} [at shorter notice, subject to the consent of the shareholders under section 101(1) of the Act] to approve the issue by special resolution, and that the notice and explanatory statement, containing the disclosures required by section 102 of the Act, Rule 13(2) of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014 and Rule 14 of the Companies (Prospectus and Allotment of Securities) Rules, 2014, be and are hereby approved.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}, be and is hereby authorised to:

(a) issue the notice of the general meeting;

(b) issue the PAS-4 letters and maintain the PAS-5 record;

(c) file Form MGT-14 for the special resolution within 30 days of its passing;

(d) obtain any required government approval under the NDI Rules; and

(e) do all acts, deeds and things necessary to give effect to these resolutions.

**Authorised to sign and file:** {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}.

> **Drafting note:** Under section 42, the offer may be made to no more than 200 persons in aggregate in a financial year (excluding qualified institutional buyers and employees under an ESOP). The Proposed Allottees must be identified in the special resolution, and no fresh offer may be made while an earlier offer is outstanding. Under the press note regime introduced by Press Note 3 (2020 Series), investment by an entity of, or a person whose beneficial owner is situated in or is a citizen of, a country that shares a land border with India requires government approval. Confirm the investor's beneficial ownership before issuing PAS-4. Rule 13 requires a registered valuer's report for a preferential issue by an unlisted company; for convertible securities, the conversion price must be determined in accordance with Rule 13 [verify]. A Board resolution under section 179(3)(c) by a private company is exempt from MGT-14 filing [verify], but the shareholders' special resolution is not. If the authorised share capital is insufficient, also pass an ordinary resolution under section 61, and file Form SH-7 within 30 days.

## 6. Authorisation of Signatories for Contracts

**Enabling provision:** section 179(1) of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Articles of Association of the Company.

**RESOLVED THAT** pursuant to section 179(1) of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Articles of Association of the Company, {{contract_signatory_names}} be and are hereby severally authorised, on behalf of the Company, to negotiate, finalise, sign, execute (including by electronic signature), deliver and amend the following, in each case up to a value of {{contract_value_limit}} per contract or series of related contracts:

(a) customer and vendor agreements, statements of work, purchase orders, non-disclosure agreements and other commercial contracts entered into in the ordinary course of business; and

(b) applications, declarations and correspondence with Governmental Authorities in connection with them.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** any contract exceeding that value, any contract outside the ordinary course of business, and any contract that is a Reserved Matter under the shareholders' agreement, will require the specific approval of the Board.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** this authority does not extend to:

(a) borrowing money, giving guarantees or creating security;

(b) the sale or disposal of any undertaking; or

(c) any other matter that, under section 179(3) or section 180 of the Act, can be exercised only by the Board at a meeting or with the approval of the shareholders.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** the company secretary or any director be and is hereby authorised to issue certified true copies of this resolution to any third party who requires it.

**Authorised to sign and file:** {{contract_signatory_names}}.

> **Drafting note:** No ROC filing is required. Where documents must be executed under the common seal (if the Company has one) or as a deed, check the Articles. Powers of attorney, documents relating to immovable property, and other instruments in the First Schedule to the Information Technology Act, 2000 cannot be executed electronically. Delegation to employees should be documented in an authority matrix that the Board reviews annually.

## 7. Change of Registered Office within the Same City

**Enabling provision:** section 12(4) of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule 27 of the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 2014.

**RESOLVED THAT** pursuant to section 12 and other applicable provisions of the Companies Act, 2013 and the rules made under it, the registered office of the Company be shifted from {{company_address}} to {{new_registered_office_address}}, being within the local limits of the same city, town or village, with effect from {{office_change_date}}.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}, be and is hereby authorised to:

(a) file Form INC-22 with the Registrar of Companies within 30 days of the change, together with proof of the new address and the owner's no-objection or the lease or rent agreement;

(b) update the address of the Company on its letterheads, invoices, website, bank accounts, GST and other tax registrations, Shops and Establishments registration, and all other licences and registrations;

(c) display the name and registered office of the Company at the new premises in accordance with section 12(3) of the Act; and

(d) do all acts, deeds and things necessary to give effect to this resolution.

**Authorised to sign and file:** {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}.

> **Drafting note:** A change within the local limits of the same city, town or village needs only a Board resolution and Form INC-22 within 30 days [verify]. Moving outside those local limits, but within the jurisdiction of the same Registrar, requires a special resolution of the shareholders (section 12(5)) and Form MGT-14. Moving to the jurisdiction of another Registrar in the same State needs Regional Director approval (Form INC-23). Moving to another State requires an alteration of the memorandum and approval of the Regional Director under section 13. The definition of "local limits" can be uncertain at municipal boundaries; check with the Registrar's practice.

## 10. Circular Resolution Format (Section 175)

**Enabling provision:** section 175 of the Companies Act, 2013 and SS-1.

**CERTIFIED TRUE COPY OF THE RESOLUTION PASSED BY THE BOARD OF DIRECTORS OF {{company_name}} (CIN: {{company_cin}}) BY CIRCULATION ON {{circulation_resolution_date}}**

**Circulation:** the draft resolution set out below, together with the necessary papers, was circulated on {{circulation_date}} to all the directors of the Company (being {{total_directors}} directors), at their addresses registered with the Company in India, by [hand delivery / post / courier / email]. Directors were asked to communicate their assent or dissent by {{circulation_response_date}}.

**Result:** the resolution was approved by {{assenting_directors}} directors, being a majority of the directors entitled to vote on it. No director required that the resolution be decided at a meeting, and the requirement under the proviso to section 175(1), that the resolution be decided at a meeting if not less than one-third of the directors so require, therefore did not apply. The resolution is deemed to have been passed on {{circulation_resolution_date}}, being the date on which the last assent required for the majority was received, or the last date for response specified in the draft, whichever is earlier.

**Resolution passed:**

**RESOLVED THAT** {{circular_resolution_text}}

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}, be and is hereby authorised to sign and file all documents and do all acts necessary to give effect to this resolution.

**RESOLVED FURTHER THAT** this resolution be noted at the next meeting of the Board and recorded in the minutes of that meeting, in accordance with section 175(2) of the Act.

**Authorised to sign and file:** {{authorised_person_name}}, {{authorised_person_designation}}.

> **Drafting note:** Under section 175 of the Act, a resolution may be passed by circulation only if the draft, with the necessary papers, is circulated to all directors at their addresses registered with the Company in India, and is approved by a majority of the directors entitled to vote. If not less than one-third of the directors require the resolution to be decided at a meeting, the chairperson must put it to a meeting. Under SS-1, the draft should specify a reasonable date for response, generally not more than 7 days from circulation, and an interested director should not vote [verify the current SS-1 text]. Matters reserved by section 179(3) (for example, issuing securities, borrowing, and approving financial statements and the Board's report), and any matter that the Act or SS-1 requires to be decided at a meeting, cannot be passed by circulation. Matters commonly passed by circulation include opening bank accounts (resolution 1), authorising contract signatories (resolution 6), changing the registered office within a city (resolution 7) and, in practice, appointing an additional director (resolution 2) [verify]. Email assent should be kept with the minutes, together with the audit trail of any electronic signature.
