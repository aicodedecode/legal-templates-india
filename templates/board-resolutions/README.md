# Board Resolutions Pack for a Private Company

**Category:** Fundraising & Corporate · **Type:** resolution · **Jurisdiction:** India · **Status:** Draft

## Overview

A pack of ten board resolutions that Indian private companies use most often, each in certified-true-copy (CTC) format consistent with the Companies Act, 2013 and the Secretarial Standard on Meetings of the Board of Directors (SS-1). Each resolution states:

- the **type and date of meeting**, or the date of passing by circulation;
- the **quorum**;
- the **enabling section**;
- **who is authorised to sign and file**.

Each is followed by a drafting note covering the related filings (for example DIR-12, PAS-3, MGT-14, INC-22, AOC-4, MGT-7A and FC-GPR) and points to check.

| # | Resolution | Main filing(s) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opening a bank account | None (bank KYC) |
| 2 | Appointment of an additional director (s.161) | DIR-12 within 30 days |
| 3 | Allotment of shares (ss.39, 42, 62(1)(c)) | PAS-3; FC-GPR for non-residents |
| 4 | ESOP plan approval and grants (s.62(1)(b), Rule 12) | MGT-14 for the special resolution; SH-6 register |
| 5 | Preferential issue / private placement offer (s.42) | PAS-4/PAS-5; MGT-14 for the special resolution |
| 6 | Authorising contract signatories (s.179(1)) | None |
| 7 | Change of registered office within the same city (s.12) | INC-22 within 30 days |
| 8 | Approval of financial statements and Board's report (s.134) | AOC-4, MGT-7/7A after the AGM |
| 9 | Appointment of the first auditor (s.139(6)) | ADT-1 if required [verify] |
| 10 | Circular resolution format (s.175) | Noted at the next Board meeting |

## When to use it

- Routine and fundraising-related Board actions of a private limited company.
- To produce CTCs for banks, investors, the Registrar of Companies, AD banks and counterparties.
- Alongside the term sheet, iSAFE and shareholders' agreement in this repository. Resolutions 3 and 5 are the closing resolutions for a priced or iSAFE round.

## When not to use it

- **Listed or public companies.** These have additional SEBI requirements, independent director and committee requirements, and wider MGT-14 filing obligations.
- **Shareholder resolutions.** Special resolutions for private placements, ESOPs and Articles amendments are passed by shareholders, and need a separate notice, explanatory statement and minutes.
- **LLPs.** These act through partner resolutions under the LLP agreement.
- **Unusual transactions** such as schemes of arrangement, buy-backs, or loans to directors (s.185). Take advice on these.

## Key provisions

- **Part A** gives a common heading (meeting type, in person or by video-conference, quorum, chairperson) and a certification block. These keep every extract consistent.
- **Resolutions 3, 4, 5 and 8** exercise powers that section 179(3) requires to be exercised **at a meeting**, so they cannot be passed by circulation.
- **Resolution 10** reproduces the section 175 mechanics:
  - circulation to all directors with the papers;
  - approval by a majority;
  - a meeting is required if one-third of the directors ask for one;
  - the resolution is noted in the minutes of the next meeting.
- **FEMA and Press Note 3** checks are built into the allotment and private placement resolutions. Allotment must happen within 60 days, with FC-GPR within 30 days of allotment.

## India-specific notes

- **MGT-14:** private companies are exempt from filing Board resolutions under section 179(3) in MGT-14, under the exemption notification of 5 June 2015 [verify]. Shareholders' special resolutions must still be filed within 30 days. This affects resolutions 4 and 5, Articles amendments and changes of registered office outside local limits.
- **Private placement timelines:**
  - allot within 60 days of receipt, or refund within the next 15 days, failing which 12% interest is payable;
  - PAS-3 within 15 days of allotment [verify];
  - at most 200 offerees per financial year;
  - a separate bank account for the application money.
- **Valuation:** a registered valuer's report is required for a preferential issue (Rule 13 of the Share Capital and Debentures Rules). For non-residents, FEMA fair value must also be certified by a chartered accountant or SEBI-registered merchant banker.
- **Dematerialisation:** private companies that are not small companies must issue securities in demat form (Rule 9B of the PAS Rules) [verify deadlines].
- **Stamp duty:** a resolution itself is not stamped. Stamp duty is payable on the issue of shares at the uniform rate introduced by the 2020 Indian Stamp Act amendments [verify], and on related agreements under State law.
- **Minutes:** minutes must be prepared within 30 days (s.118, SS-1) [verify] and kept permanently. A CTC must match the signed minutes.
- **Electronic signatures:** CTCs and ROC forms are signed with DSCs. Keep the audit trails, which support a section 63 certificate under the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 if evidence is ever needed.
- **Board's report:** must include a POSH compliance statement and, after recent amendments to the Companies (Accounts) Rules, POSH complaint statistics [verify].

## Customisation checklist

- [ ] Meeting particulars, quorum and chairperson for each resolution
- [ ] In-person or video-conference option
- [ ] Authorised person and certifying director for each resolution
- [ ] Bank signatory matrix and limits
- [ ] Director category (executive, non-executive, nominee, independent) and the DIR-2, DIR-8 and MBP-1 papers in hand
- [ ] Allotment table matches the PAS-4 letters, bank credits and valuation reports
- [ ] ESOP administrator (Board or NRC) and the Rule 12 eligibility of each grantee
- [ ] Registered office move is within local limits (if not, a special resolution is needed)
- [ ] Financial statement signatories under section 134(1), and AGM date within section 96 limits
- [ ] For circular resolutions: the matter is not a section 179(3) item, and the response window complies with SS-1
- [ ] Every form and deadline checked on the MCA portal [verify]
