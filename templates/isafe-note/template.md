# iSAFE NOTE – SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT FOR COMPULSORILY CONVERTIBLE PREFERENCE SHARES

> **Drafting note:** Why a US-style SAFE does not work in India. A SAFE is a contractual right to receive shares in the future; it is neither a share nor a debenture. In India: (a) money received by a company other than as share capital or under an exempt category may be a "deposit" under section 2(31) of the Companies Act, 2013 and the Companies (Acceptance of Deposits) Rules, 2014, and a private company may not accept deposits from the public; (b) share application money is exempt only if shares are allotted within the statutory period, and a SAFE may stay unconverted for years; and (c) FEMA allows an Indian company to receive foreign investment only through permitted instruments (equity shares, fully and mandatorily convertible preference shares and debentures, share warrants, and convertible notes issued by startups), and a SAFE is not among them. The "iSAFE" achieves SAFE-like economics (no valuation fixed today, with conversion at a cap and/or discount at the next priced round) through an immediate issue of CCPS carrying a nominal dividend. Because it is an issue of shares, it needs full private placement compliance under section 42 of the Act and, for non-residents, FEMA pricing and reporting. Defined terms match the term sheet and shareholders' agreement templates in this repository.

## 2. Subscription

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, the Investor agrees to subscribe for the CCPS, and the Company agrees to issue and allot the CCPS to the Investor, free from all encumbrances, for the Subscription Amount.

2.2 The Investor will pay the Subscription Amount within 5 Business Days after receiving the Offer Letter, by banking channels from a bank account in the Investor's own name, into the separate bank account opened by the Company solely for monies received in this private placement: {{company_bank_details}} (the **"Private Placement Account"**). No part of the Subscription Amount may be paid in cash.

2.3 The Company will not use any part of the Subscription Amount until the CCPS have been allotted and the return of allotment has been filed with the Registrar of Companies. Until then, the Subscription Amount will be held in the Private Placement Account and used only for allotment or for refund under clause 4.4.

2.4 The CCPS will rank pari passu among themselves, and with all other preference shares issued on identical terms (whether to the Investor or to any other person) under the same series, in all respects.

> **Drafting note:** Section 42 of the Act and Rule 14 of the Companies (Prospectus and Allotment of Securities) Rules, 2014 require all of the following [verify current rule text]: (a) the offer is made only to persons identified in a special resolution, and to no more than 200 persons in aggregate in a financial year (excluding qualified institutional buyers and employees under an ESOP); (b) the Offer Letter in Form PAS-4 is addressed to the Investor by name, and a record of offers is kept in Form PAS-5; (c) monies come only from the subscriber's own bank account into a separate bank account; (d) allotment is made within 60 days of receipt of the money, failing which the money is refunded within 15 days after that, with interest at 12% per annum from the 60th day; (e) the return of allotment is filed in Form PAS-3 within 15 days of allotment; and (f) no fresh offer is made while an earlier offer is outstanding. Several iSAFE investors can be closed under one special resolution, one PAS-4 per investor and a single allotment.

## 3. Conditions Precedent

3.1 The Company's obligation to allot the CCPS is conditional on the following:

(a) the Board approving this Agreement, the issue of the CCPS, the Offer Letter and the CCPS Terms, and convening a general meeting;

(b) the shareholders of the Company passing special resolutions approving (i) the issue of the CCPS by private placement on a preferential basis under sections 42 and 62(1)(c) of the Act, identifying the Investor, and (ii) any amendment to the Articles needed to create the CCPS and embody the CCPS Terms; and, where required, an ordinary resolution increasing or reclassifying the authorised share capital so that it is sufficient for both the CCPS and the Conversion Shares;

(c) the Company obtaining (i) a valuation report from a registered valuer, as required for a preferential allotment by an unlisted company under the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, and (ii) if the Investor is a person resident outside India, a valuation certificate determining the fair value of the Equity Shares under an internationally accepted pricing methodology on an arm's-length basis, issued by a chartered accountant or a SEBI-registered merchant banker (or such other valuer as FEMA permits);

(d) the Company issuing the Offer Letter to the Investor and recording it in Form PAS-5;

(e) the Investor's declaration under clause 6.2 confirming whether the land-border restriction applies and, if it does, government approval having been obtained;

(f) the representations of the Company in Schedule 2 being true, accurate and not misleading at the date of this Agreement and on the Closing Date; and

(g) the other conditions marked as Conditions Precedent in the CP and Closing Checklist in Schedule 3.

3.2 The Company will use its best efforts to satisfy the Conditions Precedent as soon as practicable, and in any event by {{long_stop_date}} (the **"Long-Stop Date"**). The Investor may waive the condition in clause 3.1(f) in writing. No other Condition Precedent may be waived where it is required by law.

3.3 If the Conditions Precedent are not satisfied by the Long-Stop Date, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice. Any part of the Subscription Amount already received will be refunded in accordance with clause 4.4.

> **Drafting note:** On the valuation date: Rule 13 of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014 requires a preferential issue by an unlisted company to be priced using a registered valuer's report. For convertible securities, Rule 13 lets the conversion price be determined either at the time of the offer or within a defined window before conversion, again based on a valuation report [verify the current text and window]. For non-residents, the NDI Rules require the conversion price or formula to be fixed at the time of issue, and the price at conversion cannot be lower than the fair value at issue. Commission both reports together, on the same valuation date, to avoid inconsistency.

## 4. Closing

4.1 Closing will take place on the date, not later than 60 days after receipt of the Subscription Amount in the Private Placement Account, on which the Board (or a duly authorised committee of the Board) passes a resolution allotting the CCPS to the Investor.

4.2 On the Closing Date, the Company will:

(a) allot the CCPS to the Investor, and enter the Investor's name in the register of members as the holder of the CCPS;

(b) issue instructions to the depository to credit the CCPS to the Investor's demat account. If the Company is permitted by law to issue certificates, it will instead issue a duly stamped share certificate within the period required by section 56(4) of the Act; and

(c) deliver to the Investor certified true copies of the Board and shareholder resolutions referred to in clause 3.1.

4.3 After Closing, the Company will complete the post-Closing actions listed in Schedule 3, including:

(a) filing Form PAS-3 within 15 days of allotment;

(b) filing Form MGT-14 for the special resolutions within 30 days of their passing;

(c) where the Investor is a person resident outside India, reporting the issue in Form FC-GPR on the FIRMS portal of the Reserve Bank of India within 30 days of allotment, together with the valuation certificate, the foreign inward remittance certificate and the KYC report of the remitter; and

(d) paying stamp duty on the issue of the CCPS.

4.4 If the CCPS are not allotted within 60 days of receipt of the Subscription Amount, the Company will refund the Subscription Amount to the Investor within 15 days after the expiry of those 60 days. If it does not, the Company will pay interest at the rate required by section 42 of the Act from the 60th day until the date of refund. In the case of a non-resident Investor, the refund will be made by banking channels in accordance with FEMA.

> **Drafting note:** Since the 2020 amendments to the Indian Stamp Act, 1899, stamp duty on the issue of shares is levied at a uniform rate (0.005% at the time of writing [verify]). It is collected through the depository for shares issued in demat form; otherwise it is paid in the State of the registered office. Private companies other than small companies must issue Securities only in demat form, and must ensure that their promoters' and directors' holdings are dematerialised before issuing further Securities (Rule 9B of the Companies (Prospectus and Allotment of Securities) Rules, 2014) [verify the current deadlines and status]. Check whether the Company has an ISIN for the CCPS well before Closing.

## 10. Conversion

10.1 The CCPS will convert into Conversion Shares in the circumstances and at the Conversion Price set out in the CCPS Terms.

10.2 On a Qualified Financing, the Investor will execute the shareholders' agreement, or a deed of adherence to it, and the other investor documents executed by the investors in the Qualified Financing. The Investor will execute them on the same terms as those investors, except for differences that arise solely from the Conversion Price, or from rights available only to investors meeting a minimum investment or holding threshold that the Investor does not meet. The Company will not agree, and the Investor will not be required, to accept terms in those documents that bind the Investor to make any further investment.

10.3 The Company will, within the time required by law:

(a) pass all Board and shareholder resolutions required for the conversion;

(b) allot the Conversion Shares and credit them to the Investor's demat account;

(c) update the register of members; and

(d) make all filings required for the conversion, including the return of allotment and any reporting of the conversion required by FEMA [verify the current form for reporting a conversion].

10.4 No fractional Conversion Share will be issued. Fractional entitlements will be rounded down.

> **Drafting note:** The FEMA floor works like this. Suppose the Issue Price equals the fair value per Equity Share at issue (the usual approach). Because the Conversion Price cannot fall below that fair value (the Floor Price), each CCPS can convert into at most one Equity Share. The cap or discount benefit therefore applies only to the extent that the Qualified Financing is priced above the Floor Price. This is usually the case in a successful next round. For resident-only iSAFE rounds there is no FEMA floor, but the conversion price must still be supported by a registered valuer's report under the Act. Run the conversion arithmetic in the cap table before fixing the Issue Price.

## Schedule 1 – CCPS Terms

1. **Issue:** the CCPS are fully paid-up, non-cumulative, compulsorily convertible preference shares, each of face value {{ccps_face_value}} and issued at the Issue Price.

2. **Dividend:** each CCPS carries a preferential, non-cumulative dividend of {{ccps_dividend_rate}} per annum on its face value, payable only if declared. If the Company declares a dividend on Equity Shares, each CCPS also participates pari passu with the Equity Shares on an as-if-converted basis.

3. **Definitions:** in these CCPS Terms:

(a) **"Qualified Financing"** means the first issue of Securities by the Company after the Closing Date, in one transaction or a series of related transactions, at a fixed pre-money valuation, in which the Company raises new money of at least {{qualified_financing_amount}}. The new money excludes any amount received on conversion of the CCPS or other convertible Securities;

(b) **"Qualified Financing Price"** means the lowest price per Equity Share, on an as-if-converted basis, paid in cash by the investors in the Qualified Financing;

(c) **"Cap Price"** means {{valuation_cap}} divided by the Fully Diluted Capitalisation;

(d) **"Discount Price"** means the Qualified Financing Price multiplied by (100% minus {{discount_rate}});

(e) **"Fully Diluted Capitalisation"** means the number of Equity Shares on a Fully Diluted Basis immediately before the relevant conversion event. It includes all Equity Shares issuable on conversion of the CCPS and of all other convertible Securities, and any increase in the ESOP Pool made in connection with the Qualified Financing. It excludes the Securities issued in the Qualified Financing itself;

(f) **"Floor Price"** means the fair value per Equity Share at the date of allotment of the CCPS, as determined in the Valuation Reports. Where the Investor is a person resident in India, it means the face value of an Equity Share, unless the Valuation Reports require otherwise;

(g) **"Conversion Price"** means the price determined under paragraph 4, 5, 6 or 7, as applicable, which will in no case be lower than the Floor Price; and

(h) **"Liquidation Event"** means:

(i) a voluntary or compulsory winding up or dissolution of the Company;

(ii) a sale, lease, exclusive licence or other disposal of all or substantially all of the assets, undertaking or intellectual property of the Company;

(iii) a merger, demerger, amalgamation, scheme of arrangement or acquisition of Securities as a result of which the shareholders of the Company immediately before that transaction hold less than 50% of the voting share capital of the surviving or acquiring entity; or

(iv) any transaction or series of related transactions resulting in a change of Control of the Company.

4. **Conversion on a Qualified Financing:** on the closing of a Qualified Financing, each CCPS will automatically convert into the number of Equity Shares equal to the Issue Price divided by the Conversion Price. For this purpose, the Conversion Price is the lower of the Cap Price and the Discount Price, but not lower than the Floor Price. Instead of Equity Shares, the Investor may, by notice given before that closing, elect to receive shares of the class issued in the Qualified Financing, if this is permitted by applicable law and the Articles and the Valuation Reports support it.

5. **Liquidation Event before a Qualified Financing:** if a Liquidation Event occurs before a Qualified Financing, the holder of each CCPS will be entitled, at its option, to either:

(a) convert the CCPS into Equity Shares at the Cap Price (but not lower than the Floor Price) immediately before the Liquidation Event; or

(b) receive, out of the proceeds available for distribution to shareholders and in priority to the holders of Equity Shares, an amount equal to the Issue Price, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

If the holder makes no election within 10 Business Days of notice of the Liquidation Event, paragraph 5(a) will apply.

6. **Qualified IPO:** all outstanding CCPS will convert at the Cap Price (but not lower than the Floor Price) before the filing of the offer document for a Qualified IPO, or at the latest time permitted by applicable law.

7. **Mandatory and optional conversion:** any CCPS not converted earlier will convert automatically into Equity Shares at the Cap Price (but not lower than the Floor Price) on the earlier of:

(a) the date falling {{conversion_long_stop_years}} years after allotment; and

(b) the last date permitted by applicable law.

The holder may also convert all or any of its CCPS at any time, at the Cap Price (but not lower than the Floor Price), by written notice to the Company in the form in Schedule 4. If the Cap Price cannot be calculated at the time of any conversion under this paragraph 7, the Conversion Price will be the Floor Price.

8. **Adjustments:** the number of Equity Shares issuable on conversion will be adjusted proportionately for any split, consolidation, bonus issue or similar reorganisation of the Equity Shares after the date of allotment.

9. **Return of capital:** on a winding up, each CCPS ranks ahead of the Equity Shares for repayment of its paid-up capital and, to the extent permitted by applicable law and the Articles, the premium paid on it. The CCPS do not participate further in any surplus.

10. **Voting:** the CCPS carry voting rights only as provided in section 47(2) of the Act, until they are converted.

11. **Conversion Shares:** the Conversion Shares will be fully paid-up and will rank pari passu with the existing Equity Shares in all respects, including dividends declared after their allotment.

12. **Transfer:** the CCPS may be transferred only in accordance with the Articles and applicable law, including FEMA pricing and reporting requirements for transfers between residents and non-residents.

13. **No redemption:** the CCPS are not redeemable. They will compulsorily convert into Equity Shares in accordance with these CCPS Terms.
