# Shareholders' Agreement (Post Priced Round)

**Category:** Fundraising & Corporate · **Type:** agreement · **Jurisdiction:** India · **Status:** Draft

## Overview

A full-form shareholders' agreement (SHA) among an Indian private limited company, its founders and its investors, for use after a priced round in which the investors subscribed to compulsorily convertible preference shares (CCPS) under a share subscription agreement (SSA). It sets out how the company is run and how shares move:

- board composition, quorum and observers
- shareholders' meetings and as-converted voting
- Reserved Matters
- information rights
- pre-emption
- transfer restrictions, ROFR and ROFO
- tag-along and drag-along
- broad-based weighted average anti-dilution
- a 1x non-participating liquidation preference
- exit through a Qualified IPO, strategic sale, buy-back or secondary sale
- founder obligations, vesting and leaver provisions
- the ESOP Pool
- covenants and Events of Default
- confidentiality, FEMA compliance and arbitration

It also contains a Deed of Adherence and a Reserved Matters schedule. Defined terms match the term sheet and iSAFE templates in this repository.

## When to use it

- After a seed or Series A/B round led by an institutional investor or angel fund in an Indian private limited company.
- Where the term sheet in this repository was used. The SHA implements its paragraphs 6–18.
- Where some investors are non-residents and the document needs to be FEMA-aware: no assured returns, pricing-compliant transfers, and Press Note 3.

## When not to use it

- **Before any outside investment.** Use a founders' agreement instead.
- **Joint ventures between corporates.** These need different deadlock, non-compete and exit mechanics.
- **Listed companies.** SEBI rules restrict special rights after listing.
- **Small angel rounds through an iSAFE.** These usually do not need a full SHA until conversion.

## Key provisions

- **Articles covenant (clause 2):** the SHA is to be written into the restated Articles by special resolution, with MGT-14 filed within 30 days. Shareholders must vote to remove any conflict between the two.
- **Board (clause 3):**
  - Nominee rights tied to shareholding thresholds, plus an observer.
  - Investor Director quorum with an adjourned-meeting fall-back.
  - Investor Director is non-executive and is not an "officer in default".
  - D&O insurance.
- **Reserved Matters (clause 5, Schedule 2):** 21 items, with deemed consent for non-fundamental items.
- **Transfers (clauses 8–12):**
  - Founder lock-in.
  - ROFR on Founder sales; ROFO for Founders before any drag.
  - Tag-along (pro rata, or full on a change of Control).
  - Drag-along subject to a time bar, a valuation floor, cash consideration and limited warranties.
- **Economics (clauses 13–14):** broad-based weighted average anti-dilution, a 1x non-participating preference, and a contractual waterfall on deemed Liquidation Events.
- **Exit (clause 15):** IPO rights (investors are not treated as promoters), strategic sale, buy-back at Fair Market Value subject to section 68, and secondary sale.
- **Founders (clause 16):**
  - Full-time engagement, with a non-compete during tenure only and a post-exit non-solicit.
  - IP assignment and moral-rights waiver.
  - Reverse vesting with Good Leaver and Bad Leaver outcomes.
- **Events of Default (clause 20):** accelerated drag, free transfer and a call on the defaulting Founder. Puts are allowed only within FEMA limits.
- **Termination on a Qualified IPO (clause 22).**
- **FEMA override (clause 23).**
- **Disputes (clause 26):** arbitration with a separately stated seat and venue, and mutual or institutional appointment. NCLT oppression remedies are preserved.

## India-specific notes

- **Articles are essential:** *V.B. Rangaraj v. V.B. Gopalakrishnan* (SC, 1992) held that share transfer restrictions not in the articles do not bind the company. *World Phone India v. WPI Group* (Del HC, 2013) applied this to SHA rights [verify]. Pass the special resolution adopting the restated Articles at closing, and file MGT-14 within 30 days [verify].
- **FEMA:**
  - Optionality clauses for non-residents must have no assured return, a minimum 1-year lock-in, and an exit price not above fair value [verify].
  - Transfers must follow the NDI Rules pricing (fair value floor or ceiling), with Form FC-TRS filed.
  - Press Note 3 (2020) approval is required for investors from countries sharing a land border with India.
  - The template routes buy-backs, puts and anti-dilution top-ups through these limits.
- **Non-compete:** post-exit restraints on founders are void under section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872. The SHA restricts competition only during tenure.
- **Shareholder remedies:** oppression and mismanagement petitions under sections 241–242 of the Companies Act, 2013 lie before the NCLT and are generally not arbitrable (*Vidya Drolia*, 2020) [verify].
- **Stamp duty:**
  - The SHA is stamped as an agreement under the law of the State where it is executed. Several States have specific entries or ad valorem rates, so check the State schedule.
  - E-stamp it before or at execution.
  - Each Deed of Adherence also needs stamping.
  - An unstamped SHA is not void, but it is inadmissible until the duty and penalty are paid (*In re Interplay*, SC, 2023).
  - Duty on the share issue itself is separate.
- **Registration:** an SHA does not need registration under the Registration Act, 1908. The amended Articles are filed with the Registrar of Companies.
- **E-signature:** permitted. Keep the platform audit trail to support a section 63 certificate under the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023. Powers of attorney cannot be signed electronically.
- **Liquidated damages and indemnity:** recoverable only as reasonable compensation (s.74 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872). Indemnities are recognised under sections 124–125.
- **Data and workplace:** the covenants refer to the DPDP Act, 2023 and DPDP Rules, 2025 (phased commencement), the CERT-In Directions (6-hour reporting), and the POSH Act, 2013.

## Customisation checklist

- [ ] Parties, founder details, and Schedule 1 cap table reconciled to the register of members
- [ ] Board size, nomination thresholds, observer, and chairperson
- [ ] Reserved Matters: delete operational items and set monetary thresholds
- [ ] Information-rights periods and threshold
- [ ] Lock-in, vesting, credit for prior service, acceleration, and Good Leaver and Bad Leaver definitions
- [ ] Non-solicit period (keep it short and narrow)
- [ ] Exit Period, drag timing and valuation floor; whether a Founder ROFO applies
- [ ] Events of Default and resident/non-resident put mechanics
- [ ] Competitor list (Schedule 5)
- [ ] Arbitration: Option A or Option B, seat and venue; who bears stamp duty
- [ ] Restated Articles drafted to mirror the SHA, and a resolution scheduled at closing
