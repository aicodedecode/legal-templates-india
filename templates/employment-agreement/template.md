# EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

> **Drafting note:** This template is drafted for an employee engaged mainly in a managerial, administrative or professional capacity, or in a supervisory capacity drawing wages above the threshold notified under the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 [verify current threshold], who is therefore not a "worker" under that Code. For employees who are "workers", the certified or model standing orders, the retrenchment and closure provisions of the Industrial Relations Code, 2020, overtime under the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 and the applicable Shops and Establishments Act override any less favourable contractual term, and Clause 22 must be adapted. Courts determine status from the dominant nature of the duties actually performed, not from the job title.

This Employment Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is made on {{effective_date}} at {{execution_place}}.

**BETWEEN**

1. **{{company_name}}**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with Corporate Identity Number {{company_cin}} and its registered office at {{company_address}} (the **"Company"**, which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include its successors and permitted assigns); and

2. **{{employee_name}}**, an adult individual residing at {{employee_address}} (the **"Employee"**).

The Company and the Employee are together the **"Parties"** and each a **"Party"**.

## RECITALS

A. The Company issued an offer of employment to the Employee dated {{offer_date}} (the **"Offer Letter"**), which the Employee has accepted.

B. The Parties wish to record the detailed terms and conditions on which the Employee is employed by the Company, in compliance with the Labour Codes, the {{shops_establishments_act}} and other applicable law.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement:

(a) **"Affiliate"** means any entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by or is under common control with the Company.

(b) **"Applicable Law"** means all laws, statutes, codes, ordinances, rules, regulations, notifications, guidelines, judgments and orders in force in India from time to time, including the Labour Codes, the {{shops_establishments_act}} and the rules made under them.

(c) **"Board"** means the board of directors of the Company.

(d) **"Business"** means the business carried on by the Company and its Affiliates from time to time, and any business that the Company is actively planning to carry on and of which the Employee is aware.

(e) **"Business Day"** means a day, other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday, on which banks are open for general business at the Company's registered office.

(f) **"Cause"** has the meaning given in Clause 17.4.

(g) **"Commencement Date"** means {{joining_date}}, being the date on which the Employee joins the Company.

(h) **"Company Property"** means all property of the Company and its Affiliates, including laptops, mobile phones, access cards, keys, credit cards, documents, records, data, source code, software, passwords, credentials, and any copies, extracts or summaries of any of them, in any medium.

(i) **"Confidential Information"** has the meaning given in Clause 12.1.

(j) **"Intellectual Property"** means patents, inventions (whether or not patentable), utility models, copyright and related rights, moral rights, trade marks, trade names, domain names, designs, rights in software, source code and object code, database rights, semiconductor layout designs, rights in know-how, trade secrets and Confidential Information, and all other intellectual property rights, in each case whether registered or unregistered, and including all applications, renewals and extensions, anywhere in the world.

(k) **"Labour Codes"** means the Code on Wages, 2019, the Industrial Relations Code, 2020, the Code on Social Security, 2020 and the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020, together with the rules, schemes and notifications made under them by the Central Government and the relevant State Government.

(l) **"Policies"** means the Company's Employee Handbook, the Prevention of Sexual Harassment Policy, the IT and acceptable use policy, the code of conduct, the anti-bribery policy and all other policies, procedures and codes issued by the Company from time to time, in each case as amended.

(m) **"Remuneration"** means the remuneration set out in Annexure A.

(n) **"Termination Date"** means the date on which the Employee's employment with the Company ends, howsoever caused.

(o) **"Wages"** has the meaning given to it in the Code on Wages, 2019.

(p) **"Work Product"** has the meaning given in Clause 13.1.

1.2 In this Agreement: (a) headings are for convenience only; (b) the singular includes the plural and vice versa; (c) references to a statute include any amendment, re-enactment or replacement of it and subordinate legislation made under it; (d) "including" means "including without limitation"; (e) references to Clauses and Annexures are to clauses of and annexures to this Agreement, and the Annexures form part of this Agreement; and (f) references to "days" are to calendar days unless stated otherwise.

1.3 This Agreement shall be read together with the Offer Letter and the Policies. In case of any conflict: (a) Applicable Law prevails over everything; (b) this Agreement prevails over the Offer Letter and the Policies; and (c) the Offer Letter prevails over the Policies.

## 2. Appointment and Commencement

2.1 The Company appoints the Employee, and the Employee accepts employment, in the position of **{{job_title}}**, with effect from the Commencement Date, on the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The Employee shall report to the {{reporting_manager}} or such other person as the Company may designate from time to time.

2.3 The Employee's appointment is conditional on: (a) satisfactory completion of background verification; (b) the Employee's submission of the documents requested by the Company; (c) the Employee being legally entitled to work in India; and (d) the accuracy of all information provided by the Employee during recruitment. If any of these conditions is not satisfied, or any information is found to be false or misleading in a material respect, the Company may terminate this Agreement under Clause 17.4.

2.4 The Employee represents and warrants that: (a) they are not bound by any agreement, undertaking, restrictive covenant or court order that would prevent or restrict them from entering into or performing this Agreement; (b) they have returned all property and confidential information of any previous employer; and (c) they will not disclose to the Company or use in their employment any confidential information or Intellectual Property belonging to any third party.

## 3. Duties and Responsibilities

3.1 The Employee shall perform the duties described in Annexure B and such other duties, consistent with their position, skills and experience, as the Company may reasonably assign from time to time. The Company may, after consultation, reasonably modify the Employee's duties, designation or reporting line in accordance with business needs, without reducing the Employee's Remuneration.

3.2 The Employee shall: (a) devote their full working time, attention and abilities to the Business; (b) faithfully and diligently perform their duties to the best of their ability; (c) comply with all lawful and reasonable directions of the Company; (d) comply with Applicable Law and the Policies; (e) promptly report to the Company any wrongdoing, fraud, breach of law or breach of the Policies of which they become aware, including by any other employee; and (f) act at all times in the best interests of the Company and in good faith.

3.3 The Employee shall not, without the prior written consent of the Company, enter into any contract or commitment on behalf of the Company or incur any expenditure, except within the limits of authority delegated to them in writing.

## 4. Place of Work, Travel and Transfer

4.1 The Employee's principal place of work is {{work_location}}.

4.2 The Employee may be required to travel within India or abroad for the purposes of the Business, in accordance with the Company's travel policy.

4.3 The Company may, on reasonable notice and after taking into account the Employee's personal circumstances, transfer the Employee to any other office, establishment or Affiliate of the Company in India, provided that the Employee's Remuneration and seniority are not adversely affected. A transfer to an Affiliate shall require the Employee's written consent.

4.4 Where the Company permits remote or hybrid working, the Employee shall comply with the Company's remote working policy, including requirements relating to information security, confidentiality and availability during working hours. The Company may withdraw or vary remote working arrangements on reasonable notice.

## 5. Working Hours

5.1 The Employee's normal working hours are {{working_hours_per_day}} hours per day (inclusive of rest intervals as required by Applicable Law) on {{working_days}}, with a weekly day of rest as required by the {{shops_establishments_act}}.

5.2 Given the managerial or professional nature of the Employee's role, the Employee may be required to work such additional hours as are reasonably necessary for the proper performance of their duties, subject to the limits on daily and weekly hours and spread-over prescribed by Applicable Law. To the extent the Employee is entitled under Applicable Law to overtime wages, overtime shall be paid at the statutory rate and only where the additional work has been authorised in advance.

5.3 Night shifts or work on weekly holidays shall be scheduled only in accordance with Applicable Law, including, for women employees, the conditions relating to consent, safety and transport prescribed under the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020 and the {{shops_establishments_act}}.

> **Drafting note:** Daily and weekly working hours, spread-over, rest intervals, weekly holidays, overtime and night work for women are regulated by the State Shops and Establishments Act and its rules (and, for establishments within its scope, the Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code, 2020). Many States exempt managerial employees from some of these provisions, or grant IT/ITES establishments exemptions by notification, but the exemptions are conditional and time-bound. Check the current State notification for the work location [verify].

## 6. Remuneration

6.1 The Company shall pay the Employee the Remuneration set out in Annexure A, the total annual cost to company being **{{annual_ctc}}**.

6.2 Fixed monthly Remuneration shall be paid in arrears by bank transfer on or before {{salary_payment_day}}, and in any event within the time limits prescribed under the Code on Wages, 2019.

6.3 The Parties acknowledge that the Wages of the Employee for the purposes of the Labour Codes comprise basic pay and any dearness allowance and retaining allowance, and that if the components excluded from Wages by the Code on Wages, 2019 exceed one half (or such other proportion as may be notified) of the Employee's total remuneration, the amount in excess shall be deemed to be Wages for the purposes of the Labour Codes.

6.4 The Company shall deduct from the Remuneration: (a) tax at source under applicable tax laws, including the Income-tax Act, 2025; (b) the Employee's contributions to provident fund, employees' state insurance and any other statutory scheme; (c) professional tax and labour welfare fund contributions, where applicable; and (d) any other deductions authorised by Applicable Law or by the Employee in writing, provided that the total deductions shall not exceed the limits prescribed by the Code on Wages, 2019.

6.5 Any variable pay, bonus or incentive (other than statutory bonus) is payable at the sole discretion of the Company in accordance with the applicable variable pay policy or plan, is not part of the Employee's guaranteed Remuneration, and, unless the policy or plan provides otherwise, is payable only if the Employee is in employment and has not given or received notice of termination on the payment date.

6.6 The Remuneration shall be reviewed annually. Any increase is at the Company's discretion and shall be communicated in writing.

6.7 If the Company overpays the Employee any amount, the Employee shall repay it, and the Company may recover it by deduction from Remuneration, to the extent permitted by the Code on Wages, 2019.

6.8 The Employee shall keep the details of their Remuneration confidential, save for disclosure to their immediate family, professional advisers and tax authorities, or as required by law. Nothing in this Clause prevents the Employee from discussing their terms of employment for the purpose of asserting their statutory rights.

## 7. Statutory Benefits

7.1 **Provident fund.** The Employee shall be enrolled in the Employees' Provident Fund and the Employees' Pension Scheme (or such other scheme as may be framed under the Code on Social Security, 2020), and the Company and the Employee shall contribute at the rates prescribed by law. If the Employee is an excluded employee on account of their Wages, membership shall be as agreed in writing between the Parties and permitted by law.

7.2 **Employees' state insurance.** If the Employee's Wages are within the ceiling notified under the Code on Social Security, 2020, the Employee shall be covered under the employees' state insurance scheme, and contributions shall be made at the prescribed rates.

7.3 **Gratuity.** The Employee shall be entitled to gratuity in accordance with Chapter V of the Code on Social Security, 2020, being (at the date of this Agreement) payable on the termination of employment after continuous service of not less than five years, or earlier on death or disablement, or, where the Employee is a fixed-term employee, on a pro rata basis in accordance with that Code, computed on the last drawn Wages at the rate and subject to the ceiling prescribed by law.

7.4 **Bonus.** If the Employee is eligible for statutory bonus under the Code on Wages, 2019 by reason of their Wages, the Company shall pay statutory bonus in accordance with that Code. Any variable pay paid under Clause 6.5 to an eligible Employee shall be treated as including statutory bonus to the extent permitted by law.

7.5 **Maternity and other benefits.** A woman Employee shall be entitled to maternity benefit, nursing breaks, crèche facilities (where applicable) and the other benefits provided under the Code on Social Security, 2020 and the Policies.

7.6 **Nominations.** The Employee shall make nominations for provident fund, gratuity, group insurance and any other benefit as required by law and keep them up to date.

> **Drafting note:** Verify current thresholds and rates before using this Agreement: the PF wage ceiling for "excluded employees", the ESI wage ceiling, the statutory bonus eligibility and calculation ceilings, and the gratuity ceiling are all set by notification and change from time to time [verify]. Under the Code on Social Security, 2020, a fixed-term employee is entitled to gratuity on a pro rata basis without completing five years; government guidance accompanying the commencement of the Codes indicates that this is available after one year of continuous service [verify against the notified Code and rules]. Maternity benefit is 26 weeks for the first two children (12 weeks thereafter) with crèche facilities for establishments above the notified headcount [verify].

## 8. Other Benefits and Expenses

8.1 The Employee shall be entitled to participate in the Company's group health insurance, group term life and personal accident insurance and other benefit schemes, if any, applicable to employees of their grade, on the terms of those schemes as in force from time to time. The Company may amend or replace any non-statutory benefit scheme on reasonable notice.

8.2 The Company shall reimburse the Employee for reasonable, documented expenses properly incurred in the performance of their duties, in accordance with the Company's expense policy.

8.3 The Employee may be eligible to be granted options under the Company's Employee Stock Option Plan. Any grant is at the discretion of the Board (or its nomination and remuneration committee) and shall be governed exclusively by the plan and the relevant grant letter. Options and any shares issued on their exercise do not form part of the Remuneration, and the treatment of options on termination is governed by the plan and not by this Agreement.

## 9. Leave and Holidays

9.1 The Employee shall be entitled, in each calendar year, to the following leave, which shall in no case be less than the entitlement under the {{shops_establishments_act}}:

(a) earned or privilege leave of {{earned_leave_days}} days, accruing proportionately for each completed month of service;

(b) casual leave of {{casual_leave_days}} days;

(c) sick leave of {{sick_leave_days}} days; and

(d) public and festival holidays as notified by the Company at the start of each year in accordance with the {{shops_establishments_act}} and the applicable national and festival holidays law of the State.

9.2 Unused earned leave may be carried forward up to a maximum accumulation of {{leave_carry_forward_cap}} days (or such higher accumulation as is required by Applicable Law), and unused earned leave shall be encashed on the Termination Date to the extent required by Applicable Law or the Policies. ## 10. Probation and Confirmation

## 11. Policies, Conduct and POSH

11.1 The Employee shall comply with the Policies, which may be amended by the Company from time to time. ## 12. Confidentiality

12.1 **"Confidential Information"** means all information, in any form, that is not publicly available and that relates to the Company, its Affiliates, the Business, or any of their customers, suppliers, investors, employees or business partners, which the Employee obtains or creates in the course of their employment, including: trade secrets; source code and technical information; product plans and roadmaps; algorithms, models and datasets; pricing, margins and financial information; customer and supplier lists and terms; sales and marketing strategies; personal data; information about investors and fundraising; and information marked or reasonably understood to be confidential.

12.2 Confidential Information does not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available other than through a breach of this Agreement; or (b) the Employee can show by written records was lawfully known to them before their employment, free of any obligation of confidence.

12.3 During and at all times after their employment, the Employee shall: (a) keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential; (b) not use the Confidential Information other than for the proper performance of their duties; (c) not disclose the Confidential Information to any person, other than in the proper performance of their duties to persons authorised by the Company; and (d) not copy, download, forward to personal accounts, upload to unauthorised applications (including generative artificial intelligence tools not approved by the Company) or remove from the Company's systems any Confidential Information, except as authorised.

12.4 Clause 12.3 does not prevent the Employee from disclosing Confidential Information to the extent required by law, regulation or an order of a court or authority, provided that (where lawful) the Employee gives the Company prompt notice and discloses only what is required, or from making a disclosure under the Company's whistleblower Policy or to a regulator or law-enforcement authority.

12.5 The obligations in this Clause 12 survive the termination of this Agreement without limit of time in respect of trade secrets and personal data, and for a period of five years after the Termination Date in respect of other Confidential Information.

## 13. Intellectual Property

13.1 **Work Product.** **"Work Product"** means all works, inventions, discoveries, developments, designs, software, source code, documentation, data, analyses, improvements, ideas and other materials, and all Intellectual Property in them, that the Employee makes, conceives, creates or develops, alone or with others, during the period of their employment, whether or not during working hours and whether or not using Company Property, which: (a) arise out of or in the course of their employment; (b) relate to the Business or to its actual or reasonably anticipated research or development; or (c) are made using the Company's equipment, facilities, Confidential Information or resources.

13.2 **Ownership.** The Parties acknowledge that, under section 17 of the Copyright Act, 1957, the Company is the first owner of copyright in works made by the Employee in the course of their employment under a contract of service. To the extent that any Work Product does not vest in the Company by operation of law, the Employee hereby assigns to the Company, absolutely and free from all encumbrances and third-party rights, by way of present assignment of present and future rights, all right, title and interest in and to all Work Product, for the full term of protection (including all renewals and extensions) and throughout the world, including all rights to reproduce, adapt, translate, publish, communicate to the public, distribute, sell, license, commercialise and otherwise exploit the Work Product in all media and formats now known or developed in the future, and the right to sue for past, present and future infringement.

13.3 **Consideration and non-lapse.** The Employee acknowledges that the Remuneration constitutes full and adequate consideration for the assignment in Clause 13.2, that no further royalty or payment is due, and that the assignment shall not lapse under section 19(4) of the Copyright Act, 1957 if the Company does not exercise the rights assigned within any particular period.

13.4 **Moral rights.** To the extent permitted by Applicable Law, the Employee waives and undertakes not to assert against the Company, its Affiliates, licensees, successors or assigns any moral rights (including those under section 57 of the Copyright Act, 1957) in the Work Product, and consents to the Company modifying, adapting and exploiting the Work Product without attribution.

13.5 **Disclosure and further assurance.** The Employee shall promptly disclose all Work Product to the Company in writing, and shall, during and after their employment, at the Company's request and expense, sign all documents (including confirmatory assignments and applications for patents, designs or other registrations) and do all acts necessary to vest, register, maintain and enforce the Company's rights in the Work Product. The Employee shall not apply for any registration of Intellectual Property in the Work Product in their own name.

13.6 **Excluded IP.** The Employee has listed in Annexure D any inventions or works they made before the Commencement Date that they wish to exclude from this Clause 13 (**"Excluded IP"**). > **Drafting note:** Section 17(c) of the Copyright Act, 1957 vests copyright in works made "in the course of" employment in the employer, but does not cover inventions (patents and designs have no employer-ownership rule in India) or works made outside the course of employment. The express present assignment in Clause 13.2 is therefore essential, and must state the rights, duration and territory to satisfy s.19 (otherwise duration defaults to five years and territory to India). The wide definition of Work Product (including work outside hours that relates to the Business) is commonly accepted but may be read down if it captures unrelated personal projects; Annexure D helps.

## 15. Restrictive Covenants

15.1 **During employment.** During their employment (including any notice period and any period of garden leave), the Employee shall not, without the prior written consent of the Company, directly or indirectly: (a) be employed by, engaged in, concerned with, or provide services to any person carrying on a business that competes with the Business; (b) solicit or deal with any customer or prospective customer of the Company for the purpose of providing competing products or services; (c) solicit, induce or encourage any employee or consultant of the Company to leave the Company; or (d) prepare to do any of the foregoing in a manner that uses Confidential Information or the Employee's working time.

15.2 **After employment – employees.** For a period of {{non_solicit_months}} months after the Termination Date, the Employee shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, induce or encourage any person who was an employee or consultant of the Company at the Termination Date, and with whom the Employee worked or about whom the Employee obtained Confidential Information in the 12 months before the Termination Date, to leave the Company.

15.3 **After employment – customers.** For a period of {{non_solicit_months}} months after the Termination Date, the Employee shall not use Confidential Information to solicit any customer of the Company with whom the Employee had material dealings in the 12 months before the Termination Date, for the purpose of supplying products or services competing with the Business.

15.4 **Non-disparagement.** The Employee shall not, during or after their employment, make any statement that is false and disparaging about the Company, its Affiliates or their directors, employees, products or services. Nothing in this Clause prevents the Employee from making truthful statements required by law, to a regulator, in legal proceedings or under the whistleblower Policy.

15.5 **Reasonableness.** The Employee acknowledges that the restrictions in this Clause 15 are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's Confidential Information, goodwill and workforce stability. Nothing in this Clause 15 restricts the Employee, after the Termination Date, from carrying on any lawful profession, trade or business, including with a competitor, provided they comply with Clauses 12, 15.2, 15.3 and 15.4.

> **Drafting note:** Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 makes void any agreement by which a person is restrained from exercising a lawful profession, trade or business. The Supreme Court has held that post-employment non-competes are void (Superintendence Co. of India v. Krishan Murgai, 1980; Percept D'Mark v. Zaheer Khan, 2006), while restraints that operate during the term of employment are valid (Niranjan Shankar Golikari v. Century Spinning, 1967). Post-employment non-solicitation of employees and customers is enforced inconsistently; courts are more willing to enforce it where it is narrow and tied to the use of confidential information (for example, Wipro Ltd v. Beckman Coulter, Delhi HC, 2006) [verify]. Do not add a post-employment non-compete, and do not rely on any supposed "reasonable duration and distance" safe harbour; none exists under Indian law.

## 18. Garden Leave

18.1 During all or any part of a notice period (whether notice is given by the Company or the Employee), the Company may direct the Employee: (a) not to perform any or some of their duties; (b) not to attend the workplace or contact customers, suppliers or employees of the Company; (c) to return all Company Property; and (d) to remain available to assist with handover (**"Garden Leave"**).

18.2 During Garden Leave, the Employee remains an employee of the Company, shall continue to receive their full fixed Remuneration and contractual benefits, shall continue to be bound by all their obligations under this Agreement (including Clauses 12 and 15.1), and shall not commence employment with any other person.

18.3 Garden Leave shall not extend beyond the end of the notice period.

> **Drafting note:** Garden leave is generally upheld in India where the employment subsists and the employee is fully paid during the notice period, because the restraint operates during the term. A garden leave clause that restrains the employee after the employment has ended has been held to be a void restraint of trade (VFS Global Services v. Suprit Roy, Bombay HC, 2008) [verify]. Keep garden leave strictly within the notice period.
