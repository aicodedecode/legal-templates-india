# {{company_name}} EMPLOYEE STOCK OPTION PLAN {{plan_year}}

> **Drafting note:** This Plan is drafted for an unlisted company (private or unlisted public) issuing new equity shares directly to employees under s.62(1)(b) of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule 12 of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014. Listed companies are governed by the SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021 instead, and this Plan must be redrafted on listing. If the company wishes to operate the Plan through an employee welfare trust, see Clause 18 and take specialist advice. Check every rule reference against the current text of Rule 12 before adoption [verify].

**Company:** {{company_name}}, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, with Corporate Identity Number {{company_cin}} and its registered office at {{company_address}}.

**Approved by the Board of Directors on:** {{board_approval_date}}

**Approved by the shareholders by special resolution on:** {{shareholder_approval_date}}

## 1. Name, Objectives and Term

1.1 This plan shall be called the **{{company_name}} Employee Stock Option Plan {{plan_year}}** (the **"Plan"**).

1.2 The objectives of the Plan are to: (a) attract, retain and reward talented Employees; (b) align the interests of Employees with those of the shareholders by enabling them to participate in the long-term growth of the Company; and (c) foster a sense of ownership among Employees.

1.3 The Plan shall come into effect on {{effective_date}} (the **"Effective Date"**) and shall continue in force for {{plan_term_years}} years, unless terminated earlier under Clause 20. Termination or expiry of the Plan shall not affect Options already granted, which shall continue to be governed by the Plan.

## 2. Definitions and Interpretation

2.1 In this Plan, unless the context otherwise requires:

(a) **"Act"** means the Companies Act, 2013 and the rules made under it, including the SCD Rules.

(b) **"Administrator"** means the {{administrator_name}}, or, if no such committee is constituted, the Board.

(c) **"Applicable Law"** means all laws, statutes, rules, regulations, notifications, circulars and guidelines in force in India from time to time that are relevant to the Plan, including the Act, the SCD Rules, the Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999 and the rules and regulations made under it, the Income-tax Act, 2025 and the rules made under it, and applicable accounting standards.

(d) **"Articles"** means the articles of association of the Company, as amended from time to time.

(e) **"Board"** means the board of directors of the Company.

(f) **"Cause"** means, in relation to a Grantee: (i) fraud, dishonesty, embezzlement or misappropriation; (ii) conviction for an offence involving moral turpitude; (iii) a material breach of the Grantee's employment agreement, the Plan, the Grant Letter or the Company's policies (including any confidentiality, intellectual property or non-solicitation obligation); (iv) gross misconduct, wilful negligence or wilful disobedience of lawful orders; or (v) any act or omission that results in termination of employment by the Company for misconduct after an inquiry under its disciplinary procedure; in each case as determined by the Administrator.

(g) **"Change of Control"** means (i) the acquisition by any person, or persons acting in concert, of more than 50% of the voting share capital of the Company or the right to appoint a majority of the Board; (ii) a merger, amalgamation, demerger or scheme of arrangement under which the shareholders of the Company immediately before it hold less than 50% of the voting share capital of the resulting entity; or (iii) the sale, lease or transfer of all or substantially all of the assets or undertaking of the Company.

(h) **"Company"** means {{company_name}}.

(i) **"Employee"** has the meaning given in Clause 6.1.

(j) **"Exercise"** means the making of an application by a Grantee to the Company, in accordance with Clause 10, for the issue of Shares against Vested Options, and "Exercised" shall be construed accordingly.

(k) **"Exercise Notice"** means a notice of exercise substantially in the form set out in Schedule 2.

(l) **"Exercise Period"** means the period within which Vested Options may be Exercised, as set out in Clause 10.1 or Clause 13.

(m) **"Exercise Price"** means the price payable by a Grantee for each Share on Exercise of an Option, as specified in the Grant Letter, subject to adjustment under Clause 14.

(n) **"Fair Market Value"** means the fair market value of a Share as determined by a registered valuer or a SEBI-registered merchant banker (as required by Applicable Law for the relevant purpose) on a date not earlier than 180 days before the relevant event, or such other value as is required by Applicable Law for the relevant purpose.

(o) **"Grant"** means the issue of Options to an Employee under the Plan, and **"Grant Date"** means the date of the Grant specified in the Grant Letter.

(p) **"Grant Letter"** means a letter issued by the Company to an Employee substantially in the form set out in Schedule 1, specifying the terms of a Grant.

(q) **"Grantee"** means an Employee to whom Options have been Granted and who has accepted the Grant, and, where the context requires, includes the Grantee's legal heirs or nominees under Clause 13.

(r) **"Group Company"** means any subsidiary or holding company of the Company, within the meaning of the Act, whether incorporated in India or outside India.

(s) **"Liquidity Event"** means (i) a Change of Control; (ii) the listing of the Shares (or of the shares of any successor entity) on a recognised stock exchange; or (iii) any other event designated by the Board as a liquidity event, including a structured secondary sale or buyback in which Grantees are permitted to participate.

(t) **"Option"** means the option given to an Employee under the Plan, which gives the Employee the right, but not the obligation, to apply for and be issued Shares at the Exercise Price, on the terms of the Plan and the relevant Grant Letter.

(u) **"Permanent Incapacity"** means any disability, of whatever nature, be it physical, mental or otherwise, which incapacitates or prevents or handicaps a Grantee from performing any specific job, work or task which the Grantee was capable of performing immediately before such disability, as determined by the Administrator on the basis of a certificate of a medical expert identified by it.

(v) **"Pool"** has the meaning given in Clause 4.1.

(w) **"SCD Rules"** means the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, as amended.

(x) **"Shares"** means fully paid-up equity shares of the Company of face value {{share_face_value}} each.

(y) **"Unvested Option"** means an Option that has not vested in accordance with Clause 8, and **"Vested Option"** means an Option that has vested in accordance with Clause 8 (or Clause 13 or 14) and has not lapsed or been Exercised.

(z) **"Vesting"** means the process by which a Grantee becomes entitled to Exercise an Option, and **"Vesting Date"** and **"Vesting Period"** shall be construed accordingly.

2.2 In this Plan: (a) headings are for convenience only; (b) the singular includes the plural and vice versa; (c) references to a statute or rule include any amendment, re-enactment or replacement of it; (d) "including" means "including without limitation"; (e) references to Clauses and Schedules are to clauses of and schedules to this Plan, and the Schedules form part of this Plan; and (f) terms not defined in this Plan but defined in the Act or the SCD Rules have the meanings given to them there.

2.3 If any provision of this Plan is inconsistent with Applicable Law, Applicable Law shall prevail, and the Administrator may make such changes to the Plan's operation as are necessary to comply with it.

## 3. Authority and Approvals

3.1 The Plan has been approved by the Board on {{board_approval_date}} and by the shareholders of the Company by way of a special resolution passed on {{shareholder_approval_date}}, in accordance with section 62(1)(b) of the Act and Rule 12 of the SCD Rules. The explanatory statement annexed to the notice of the general meeting contained the disclosures required by Rule 12 of the SCD Rules.

3.2 The Company shall obtain the separate approval of its shareholders by special resolution before: (a) Granting Options to employees of a Group Company; or (b) Granting Options to any identified Employee, during any one year, equal to or exceeding 1% of the issued capital of the Company (excluding outstanding warrants and conversions) at the time of the Grant.

3.3 The Company shall file the special resolution(s) approving the Plan with the Registrar of Companies within the time prescribed under the Act.

> **Drafting note:** By a notification dated 5 June 2015 exempting private companies from certain provisions, the Ministry of Corporate Affairs provided that, for private companies, s.62(1)(b) may be complied with by an ordinary resolution [verify]. Rule 12 of the SCD Rules nevertheless refers to a special resolution, and the safer and more common practice, expected by investors in due diligence, is to pass a special resolution and file Form MGT-14 within 30 days [verify]. Schedule 3 sets out the resolution and the Rule 12 explanatory statement disclosures.

## 4. Pool

4.1 The maximum number of Options that may be Granted under the Plan is {{pool_size}} (the **"Pool"**), representing approximately {{pool_percentage}} of the share capital of the Company on a fully diluted basis as at the Effective Date. Each Option entitles the Grantee to one Share on Exercise.

4.2 The Pool shall be adjusted proportionately for any corporate action under Clause 14.

4.3 Options that lapse, are forfeited, are cancelled or expire without being Exercised shall be added back to the Pool and may be re-Granted, within the overall limit in Clause 4.1 and the term of the Plan.

4.4 Any increase in the Pool requires the approval of the shareholders by special resolution as a variation of the Plan.

## 5. Administration

5.1 The Plan shall be administered by the Administrator, which shall have full power and authority, subject to the Act, the SCD Rules, the Articles and this Plan, to:

(a) identify the Employees eligible to participate and determine the number of Options to be Granted to each, having regard to the criteria in Clause 6.3;

(b) determine the Grant Date, Exercise Price, Vesting schedule, any performance conditions, and the other terms of each Grant;

(c) determine the procedure for Exercise, including Exercise windows and the method of payment;

(d) determine the treatment of Options on a Grantee's cessation of employment, within the limits of Clause 13;

(e) make adjustments under Clause 14 on a corporate action;

(f) interpret the Plan and any Grant Letter, and decide any question or dispute regarding the Plan;

(g) frame, amend and rescind rules and procedures for the operation of the Plan; and

(h) delegate any ministerial or administrative function to any officer of the Company.

5.2 Every decision of the Administrator within its authority shall be final and binding on the Company and all Grantees, subject to Clause 22.

5.3 No member of the Administrator shall participate in any decision regarding a Grant to themselves or to their relative.

5.4 No member of the Administrator or the Board shall be liable for any act or omission in good faith in connection with the Plan.

## 6. Eligibility

6.1 An **"Employee"** means, in accordance with Rule 12 of the SCD Rules:

(a) a permanent employee of the Company who has been working in India or outside India;

(b) a director of the Company, whether a whole-time director or not, but excluding an independent director; or

(c) an employee, as described in sub-clause (a) or (b), of a Group Company in India or outside India,

but does not include:

(i) an employee who is a promoter or a person belonging to the promoter group; or

(ii) a director who, either by themselves or through their relative or through any body corporate, directly or indirectly, holds more than 10% of the outstanding equity shares of the Company,

provided that, for so long as the Company is a "startup company" as defined in the SCD Rules (being a company recognised as a startup by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade), the exclusions in sub-clauses (i) and (ii) shall not apply up to ten years from the date of its incorporation or registration.

6.2 Consultants, advisors, contractors, interns, and independent directors are not eligible to receive Options under the Plan.

6.3 In identifying eligible Employees and determining the number of Options, the Administrator may consider: the Employee's role, grade and seniority; length of service; performance and potential; criticality of the role and of retention; contribution to the Company; market practice; and such other criteria as it considers relevant. No Employee shall have any right or claim to be Granted Options.

6.4 The maximum number of Options that may be Granted to any one Employee in any financial year is {{max_options_per_employee}}, unless the shareholders approve a higher number in accordance with Clause 3.2.

> **Drafting note:** The ten-year promoter and >10% shareholder exception applies only to companies that hold DPIIT recognition as a "startup" under the government notification in force from time to time; DPIIT recognition is itself time-limited and turnover-limited. Confirm and record the Company's DPIIT certificate and date of incorporation before granting to a founder or significant shareholder, and track the date on which the exception ceases [verify]. Advisors and consultants cannot receive options under s.62(1)(b); consider sweat equity (s.54), phantom stock or a cash-settled stock appreciation right instead. The Plan definition must match Rule 12 exactly; do not widen it.

## 7. Grant of Options

7.1 Each Grant shall be made by the Administrator and communicated to the Employee by a Grant Letter specifying: (a) the number of Options Granted; (b) the Grant Date; (c) the Exercise Price; (d) the Vesting schedule and any performance conditions; (e) the Exercise Period; and (f) any other terms determined by the Administrator.

7.2 The Employee shall accept the Grant by signing and returning the Grant Letter (including electronically) within {{grant_acceptance_days}} days of its receipt. If the Grant is not accepted within that period, it shall lapse and the Options shall be added back to the Pool.

7.3 No amount shall be payable by an Employee on the Grant of Options.

7.4 The Grant of Options does not confer on the Grantee any right to continued employment, and does not form part of the Grantee's remuneration, wages or salary for any purpose, including for the computation of gratuity, provident fund, bonus, notice pay or compensation on termination.

## 8. Vesting

8.1 Options shall vest in accordance with the Vesting schedule set out in the Grant Letter, subject to the Grantee remaining in continuous employment with the Company or a Group Company on each Vesting Date and to the satisfaction of any performance conditions.

8.2 There shall be a minimum period of one year between the Grant Date and the first Vesting Date of any Option. No Option shall vest before the expiry of one year from its Grant Date, except as provided in Clause 13.3 (death) or Clause 13.4 (Permanent Incapacity) or as otherwise permitted by Applicable Law.

8.3 Unless the Grant Letter provides otherwise, Options shall vest over a period of {{vesting_period_months}} months from the vesting commencement date specified in the Grant Letter, with no vesting during the first {{cliff_months}} months, the proportion attributable to that period vesting at its end, and the balance vesting in equal quarterly instalments over the remainder of the Vesting Period, provided that in every case Clause 8.2 is satisfied.

8.4 The Administrator may specify performance conditions for Vesting in the Grant Letter, in which case it shall determine, within a reasonable time after the relevant Vesting Date, whether and to what extent those conditions have been satisfied.

8.5 A period of approved leave (including statutory maternity leave) shall not interrupt continuous employment for the purposes of Vesting. The Administrator may determine whether and how Vesting continues during an unpaid sabbatical or extended leave of absence.

> **Drafting note:** The one-year minimum is between the grant date and vesting, not the date of joining or a back-dated vesting commencement date. A Grant Letter can give credit for service before the grant (for example, by fixing the vesting commencement date at the joining date) but nothing can vest within twelve months of the Grant Date itself. Grants made long after joining are therefore common in practice in Indian startups, and the gap should be managed by granting promptly after the plan is approved.

## 9. Exercise Price

9.1 The Exercise Price shall be determined by the Administrator at the time of Grant and specified in the Grant Letter. The Exercise Price may be at, below or above the Fair Market Value of a Share on the Grant Date, but shall not be less than the face value of a Share.

9.2 The Company shall comply with the applicable accounting standards in respect of the Options, including the recognition of the employee compensation cost.

> **Drafting note:** Rule 12 gives unlisted companies freedom to determine the exercise price, subject to conforming to the applicable accounting policies. Shares cannot be issued at a discount to face value (s.53 of the Companies Act, 2013, other than sweat equity). Where the exercise price is set at face value, the entire difference between Fair Market Value on exercise and the exercise price is taxable as a perquisite, so communicate this to Grantees. If any Grantee is a non-resident, check the pricing requirements under the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019 [verify].

## 10. Exercise

10.1 **Exercise Period.** Subject to Clause 13 and Clause 14, a Vested Option may be Exercised at any time within {{exercise_period_years}} years from its Vesting Date (or such other period as the Grant Letter may specify), during such Exercise windows as the Administrator may prescribe. Any Vested Option not Exercised within the Exercise Period shall lapse without compensation.

10.2 **Procedure.** A Grantee may Exercise Vested Options, in whole or in part (but not for a fraction of a Share), by delivering to the Company a duly completed Exercise Notice together with:

(a) payment of the aggregate Exercise Price by bank transfer to the Company's designated account;

(b) payment of, or satisfactory arrangements for, any tax required to be deducted or collected by the Company in respect of the Exercise under Clause 15;

(c) where required by the Company, a deed of adherence to any shareholders' agreement to which the Company is party, and such other documents as the Company may reasonably require; and

(d) where the Grantee is a non-resident, all documents required to comply with Applicable Law relating to foreign exchange.

10.3 **Allotment.** Subject to receipt of the items in Clause 10.2, the Company shall allot the relevant Shares to the Grantee within 60 days of the Exercise Notice, credit them to the Grantee's demat account (or, if permitted, issue a share certificate), file the return of allotment with the Registrar of Companies and make the corresponding entries in the register of members and the register of employee stock options.

10.4 **Ranking.** Shares issued on Exercise shall rank pari passu in all respects with the existing Shares, including as to dividends declared after the date of allotment.

10.5 **Liquidity Event exercise.** On or in anticipation of a Liquidity Event, the Administrator may require Grantees to Exercise all Vested Options within a specified period before completion, failing which those Vested Options shall lapse, or may permit Grantees to Exercise and sell simultaneously, or may cancel Vested Options against payment of their intrinsic value (the price per Share in the Liquidity Event less the Exercise Price), net of tax, in accordance with Applicable Law.

10.6 **Restriction.** The Company shall not be obliged to issue Shares if the issue would breach Applicable Law, the Articles or any approval required from a regulator, in which case the Exercise Period shall be extended for so long as the restriction continues.

## 11. Shares Issued on Exercise

11.1 **Articles and shareholder agreements.** Shares issued on Exercise shall be subject to the Articles and to the provisions of any shareholders' agreement to which the Company is party that apply to holders of Shares generally, including restrictions on transfer, rights of first refusal, tag-along and drag-along rights.

11.2 **Lock-in.** Shares issued on Exercise shall be subject to a lock-in period of {{esop_lock_in_months}} months from the date of allotment (if greater than zero), during which they may not be transferred except under a drag-along, a Liquidity Event, or with the Board's consent, provided that the lock-in shall not apply to Shares sold to pay tax arising on Exercise, to the extent permitted.

11.3 **No shareholder rights before allotment.** A Grantee shall have no right to receive any dividend, to vote or to enjoy any other benefit of a shareholder in respect of any Option until Shares are allotted on its Exercise.

11.4 **Market stand-off.** In connection with an initial public offering, each Grantee shall comply with any lock-in on Shares required by Applicable Law or by the underwriters on the same terms as other shareholders.

## 12. Non-Transferability

12.1 Options are personal to the Grantee. During the Grantee's lifetime, only the Grantee may Exercise the Options.

12.2 Options shall not be transferred, pledged, hypothecated, mortgaged, encumbered or otherwise alienated in any manner. Any purported transfer or encumbrance shall be void, and the relevant Options shall lapse.

12.3 Clause 12.2 does not prevent the transmission of Options to the legal heirs or nominees of a deceased Grantee under Clause 13.3.

## 13. Cessation of Employment

13.1 **Resignation or termination without Cause.** If a Grantee ceases to be an Employee by reason of resignation, or termination of employment by the Company other than for Cause:

(a) all Unvested Options on the date of cessation shall lapse immediately, unless the Administrator determines otherwise; and

(b) Vested Options may be Exercised within {{leaver_exercise_days}} days of the date of cessation (or, if earlier, the expiry of the Exercise Period), failing which they shall lapse. [OPTION: Vested Options may instead be Exercised up to and including the occurrence of a Liquidity Event, subject to the overall term of the Plan.]

13.2 **Termination for Cause.** If a Grantee's employment is terminated for Cause, all Options (both Vested and Unvested) shall lapse immediately on the date of termination (or, if earlier, on the date of the Grantee's suspension pending an inquiry, subject to reinstatement if the Grantee is exonerated).

13.3 **Death.** If a Grantee dies while in employment, all Options Granted to the Grantee shall vest in the Grantee's legal heirs or nominees on the date of death, and may be Exercised by them within 12 months of the date of death (or such longer period as the Administrator may permit within the Exercise Period).

13.4 **Permanent Incapacity.** If a Grantee suffers Permanent Incapacity while in employment, all Options Granted to the Grantee as on the date of Permanent Incapacity shall vest in the Grantee on that date, and may be Exercised within 12 months of that date.

13.5 **Retirement.** If a Grantee retires on reaching the retirement age under the Company's policy, or retires early with the approval of the Company, Vested Options may be Exercised within the period in Clause 13.1(b), and the Administrator may in its discretion permit Unvested Options to continue to vest on their original schedule.

13.6 **Transfer within the group.** A transfer of employment from the Company to a Group Company, or vice versa, shall not be a cessation of employment for the purposes of this Plan, provided that the Grantee continues to be an Employee.

13.7 **Abandonment.** If a Grantee abandons employment without notice, they shall be treated as having resigned on the last day they attended work, unless the Administrator determines that the circumstances constitute Cause.

> **Drafting note:** Rule 12 requires that on the death of an employee all options granted vest in the legal heirs or nominees, and on permanent incapacity all options granted vest in the employee, and that on resignation or termination options not vested lapse while vested options may be exercised within the period specified in the scheme [verify current text]. Clauses 13.3 and 13.4 apply these rules even where the one-year minimum vesting period has not run. The forfeiture of vested options on termination for Cause (Clause 13.2) is common but may be challenged if "Cause" is disputed; ensure the employment disciplinary process is followed. A short post-exit exercise window (often 90 days) forces leavers to pay exercise price and tax on illiquid shares; many startups now permit exercise until a Liquidity Event (see the OPTION in Clause 13.1(b)).

## 14. Corporate Actions

14.1 **Capital changes.** If the Company makes a bonus issue, sub-division or consolidation of Shares, rights issue or other change in its capital structure, the Administrator shall make such fair and equitable adjustments to the number of Options (Vested and Unvested), the Exercise Price and the Pool as are necessary to ensure that the value of the Options is neither diluted nor enhanced, in accordance with Applicable Law and accounting standards.

14.2 **Merger and Change of Control.** On a merger, amalgamation, demerger, scheme of arrangement or Change of Control, the Administrator may, in its discretion and subject to Applicable Law and the terms of the transaction:

(a) arrange for the Options to be assumed or substituted by equivalent options in the acquirer or resulting entity, on terms no less favourable to Grantees;

(b) accelerate the Vesting of all or part of the Unvested Options, subject to the minimum Vesting period of one year from the Grant Date, effective immediately before completion;

(c) require Vested Options to be Exercised before completion under Clause 10.5; or

(d) cancel Options against a cash payment equal to their intrinsic value, net of applicable taxes.

14.3 **Winding up.** On the winding up of the Company, all Options not Exercised before the commencement of the winding up shall lapse.

14.4 **Listing.** If the Company proposes to list its Shares, the Administrator shall make such amendments to the Plan as are required by the SEBI (Share Based Employee Benefits and Sweat Equity) Regulations, 2021, subject to shareholder approval where required, and the Plan as amended shall continue to apply to outstanding Options.

## 15. Taxes

15.1 The Grantee shall be liable for all taxes arising in connection with the Options and the Shares, including the tax on the perquisite value at the time of allotment of Shares on Exercise (being, in general, the Fair Market Value of the Shares on the date of Exercise less the Exercise Price paid) and any tax on capital gains on the subsequent sale of the Shares.

15.2 The Company shall deduct or collect tax at source as required by applicable tax laws, including the Income-tax Act, 2025. The Company may recover the tax from the Grantee's salary or other amounts payable, require the Grantee to pay it in cash before allotment, or, with the Grantee's consent and subject to Applicable Law, withhold or arrange the sale of a sufficient number of Shares.

15.3 Where the Company is an eligible startup entitled under applicable tax laws to defer the deduction or payment of tax on the perquisite value, the Company shall apply such deferral in accordance with Applicable Law, and the tax shall become payable on the earliest of the events specified in the law (which, at the date of this Plan, include the expiry of a specified period from the end of the relevant assessment year, the sale of the Shares, and the Grantee ceasing to be employed by the Company).

15.4 The Company makes no representation as to the tax treatment of the Options or the Shares. Each Grantee shall take their own tax advice, including advice on tax in any jurisdiction outside India in which they are resident.

> **Drafting note:** The Income-tax Act, 2025 has replaced the Income-tax Act, 1961 from 1 April 2026; do not cite section numbers of either Act in the Plan.
