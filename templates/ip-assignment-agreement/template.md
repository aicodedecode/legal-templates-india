# INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT AGREEMENT

> **Drafting note:** Use this standalone agreement to confirm and perfect the Company's title to intellectual property created by a founder, employee, consultant or advisor, in particular: (a) a founder's pre-incorporation work (code, designs, brand, domain names) that the Company now uses; (b) an employee or contractor who did not sign an adequate assignment when engaged, or whose earlier assignment did not satisfy section 19 of the Copyright Act, 1957; and (c) a "clean-up" before a fundraise or acquisition, where investors' due diligence requires a complete chain of title. It operates both as a present assignment and as a confirmation of earlier assignments. It does not replace the IP clauses of an employment, consultancy or founders' agreement; it supplements them. Delete all drafting notes before signing.

This Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is made on {{effective_date}} (the **"Effective Date"**)

**BETWEEN**

**{{assignor_name}}**, an individual of Indian nationality, residing at {{assignor_address}} (the **"Assignor"**, which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof, include the Assignor's legal heirs, legal representatives, executors and administrators);

**AND**

**{{company_name}}**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 with corporate identity number {{company_cin}}, having its registered office at {{company_address}} (the **"Company"**, which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof, include its successors and assigns).

The Assignor and the Company are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

> **Drafting note:** If the Assignor is not an Indian national, replace "of Indian nationality" with the Assignor's nationality and check whether the Assignor's home country requires any formality for an assignment of rights in works or inventions created there (for example, notarisation or a separate country-specific form). If the person who created the work was employed or engaged by another entity at the time (including a university, research institute or former employer), that entity may own the rights; obtain a release or assignment from it as well.

(a) **"Affiliate"** means, in relation to the Company, its holding company, its subsidiaries and the subsidiaries of its holding company, in each case within the meaning of the Companies Act, 2013, from time to time;

(b) **"Applicable Law"** means all laws, statutes, rules, regulations, notifications, directions, orders and judgments of any governmental, statutory, regulatory, judicial or quasi-judicial authority, in India or elsewhere, that apply to a Party or to the Assigned IP;

(c) **"Assigned IP"** means: (i) the Assigned Works; (ii) the Inventions; (iii) the Company Marks; (iv) the Know-how; and (v) all Intellectual Property Rights in any of them, in each case excluding the Excluded Inventions;

(d) **"Assigned Works"** means all works (including literary works, computer programs and source code and object code, databases, tables and compilations, documentation, artistic works, logos, graphics, user interface designs, photographs, cinematograph films, sound recordings, website and app content, presentations, pitch decks, business plans, specifications and prompts) that the Assignor has created or creates, alone or jointly with others, at any time (whether before or after the Effective Date and whether before or after the incorporation of the Company) that: (A) relate to the Business or to the actual or demonstrably anticipated research, development, products or services of the Company; (B) were or are created in the course of the Engagement; (C) were or are created using the equipment, facilities, funds, Confidential Information or other resources of the Company; or (D) are described in Schedule 1;

(f) **"Company Marks"** means all trade marks, service marks, trade names, brand names, logos, domain names, social-media handles and accounts, app-store listings and other identifiers that the Assignor has adopted, registered, applied for or used, at any time, for or in connection with the Business or the Company, including those described in Schedule 1, together with the goodwill of the Business associated with them;

(g) **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information relating to the Company, the Business or the Assigned IP, including source code, algorithms, models, data, product plans, customer information and the Know-how;

(h) **"Consideration"** has the meaning given in Clause 2.1;

(i) **"Engagement"** means each relationship, past, present or future, under which the Assignor has provided or provides services to or for the Company or the Business, whether as a founder, promoter, director, employee, consultant, advisor, intern or otherwise, and whether or not under a written agreement, and **"Engagement Period"** means the total period of the Engagement;

(j) **"Excluded Inventions"** means the inventions, works and other subject matter owned by the Assignor (alone or with others) before the commencement of the Engagement that are listed in Schedule 2, and that are expressly excluded from this Agreement;

(k) **"Intellectual Property Rights"** means copyright and related rights (including all rights comprised in copyright under section 14 of the Copyright Act, 1957), patents, utility models, rights in inventions, the right to apply for and obtain patents, registered and unregistered designs, trade marks and service marks (whether registered or not), trade names, domain names, rights in get-up, goodwill and the right to sue for passing off, rights in computer software, database rights, rights in confidential information (including know-how and trade secrets), semiconductor integrated circuit layout-design rights, and all other intellectual property rights, in each case whether registered or unregistered, including all applications for, and renewals, extensions, reissues, divisions, continuations and revivals of, such rights, all rights of priority and the right to claim priority, and all similar or equivalent rights anywhere in the world;

(l) **"Inventions"** means all inventions, discoveries, improvements, processes, methods, formulae, algorithms, models, techniques, designs and developments (whether or not patentable or registrable) that the Assignor has conceived, developed or reduced to practice, or conceives, develops or reduces to practice, alone or jointly with others, at any time, that satisfy any of the conditions in paragraphs (A) to (D) of the definition of Assigned Works;

## 2. Consideration

2.1 In consideration of the assignment and other covenants of the Assignor under this Agreement, the Company has agreed to provide the Assignor with the following: {{assignment_consideration}} (the **"Consideration"**).

2.2 The Assignor acknowledges that the Consideration, together with the benefits the Assignor has received or will receive from the Engagement, is full, adequate and sufficient consideration for the assignment of all the Assigned IP and for the purposes of section 19(3) of the Copyright Act, 1957, and that no royalty, fee, share of profits or other amount is or will become payable to the Assignor, or to any person claiming through the Assignor, for the Assigned IP or its exploitation by the Company or its successors, licensees or assigns.

> **Drafting note:** An agreement without consideration is void under section 25 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 (subject to limited exceptions), and section 19(3) of the Copyright Act, 1957 requires the assignment to specify the royalty and any other consideration payable. Indian law recognises past consideration (section 2(d)), so prior salary or fees may support the assignment, but stating a specific present consideration avoids argument. Options include: (a) a nominal cash sum (for example, ₹ 1,000); (b) for an employee or consultant, a stated amount together with the continuation of the Engagement; or (c) for a founder, the allotment of shares. If shares are to be allotted for the IP, that is an allotment for consideration other than cash, which requires compliance with the Companies Act, 2013 and the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014 or the Companies (Prospectus and Allotment of Securities) Rules, 2014 as applicable, including a valuation report from a registered valuer and disclosure in the return of allotment [verify the current requirements for non-cash allotments]. Where the Assignor is a director (or relative of a director) and the Company is acquiring property from them, consider the related party transaction requirements under section 188 and the disclosure of interest under section 184 of the Companies Act, 2013, and record board approval [verify whether the transaction falls within section 188(1) and the applicable thresholds]. Where the Company pays cash for the IP, deduct tax at source if required under applicable tax laws.

## 3. Assignment

3.1 **Present assignment.** The Assignor hereby assigns, transfers and conveys to the Company, absolutely and unconditionally, with full title guarantee and free from all liens, charges, encumbrances, licences and third-party rights, all of the Assignor's right, title and interest, present and future, legal and beneficial, in and to the Assigned IP, together with:

(a) all goodwill attaching to the Company Marks and to the Business;

(b) all rights of action, powers and remedies in respect of any past, present or future infringement, misappropriation or other violation of any of the Assigned IP, including the right to sue for and retain damages, accounts of profits and other relief in respect of infringements occurring before the Effective Date;

(c) the right to apply for, prosecute and obtain registration of any Intellectual Property Rights in the Assigned IP in the name of the Company in any country, and all rights of priority arising from any application filed by or on behalf of the Assignor; and

(d) all Records.

3.2 **Copyright particulars.** For the purposes of section 19 of the Copyright Act, 1957, the Parties record that:

(a) **Works:** the works assigned are each and every Assigned Work, including (without limitation) the works identified in Part A of Schedule 1;

(b) **Rights:** the rights assigned are the entire copyright in each Assigned Work and all rights comprised in it under section 14 of the Copyright Act, 1957, including, as applicable to the nature of the work, the exclusive rights: to reproduce the work in any material form, including the storing of it in any medium by electronic or other means; to issue copies of the work to the public; to perform the work in public or communicate it to the public; to make any cinematograph film or sound recording in respect of the work; to make any translation or adaptation of the work, and to do any of the foregoing acts in relation to a translation or adaptation; in the case of a computer program, to sell or give on commercial rental or offer for sale or for commercial rental any copy of it; and in the case of an artistic work, to depict it in three dimensions; together with the right to license, sub-license, modify, combine, commercialise and otherwise exploit the Assigned Works by any means and in any medium now known or later devised;

(c) **Duration:** the assignment is for the full term of copyright in each Assigned Work, including all renewals, revivals, reversions and extensions, and thereafter so far as any right subsists;

(d) **Territory:** the assignment extends to the whole world; and

(e) **Consideration:** the consideration for the assignment is the Consideration described in Clause 2.

3.3 **Future works.** To the extent that any Assigned Work or other Assigned IP comes into existence after the Effective Date, the Assignor hereby assigns to the Company, by way of present assignment of future rights, all such rights, and the Parties acknowledge that, in accordance with the proviso to section 18(1) of the Copyright Act, 1957, the assignment of copyright in any such future work shall take effect automatically, without any further act, when the work comes into existence.

3.4 **No lapse or reversion.** Notwithstanding section 19(4) of the Copyright Act, 1957, the rights assigned under this Agreement shall not lapse or revert to the Assignor by reason of the Company not exercising them within one year of the assignment or within any other period. The duration and territory of the assignment are expressly stated in Clause 3.2, and accordingly sections 19(5) and 19(6) of that Act (which deem an assignment to be for five years and limited to India where the duration or territory is not stated) do not apply.

3.5 **Patents and inventions.** Without limiting Clause 3.1, the Assignor assigns to the Company all rights in the Inventions, including the right to apply for and obtain patents for the Inventions in India under the Patents Act, 1970 and in every other country, and all patents and patent applications claiming any Invention, whether filed before or after the Effective Date and whether filed in the name of the Assignor or otherwise. The Parties intend this Agreement to be an assignment in writing, duly executed, for the purposes of section 68 of the Patents Act, 1970, and to constitute proof of the Company's right to make an application for a patent as assignee of the right to apply, for the purposes of section 7(2) of that Act.

3.6 **Designs.** Without limiting Clause 3.1, the Assignor assigns to the Company all rights in any design comprised in the Assigned IP, including the right to apply for registration of it under the Designs Act, 2000 and the corresponding laws of other countries, and the copyright in any such design.

3.7 **Trade marks and domain names.** Without limiting Clause 3.1, the Assignor assigns to the Company all rights in the Company Marks, together with the goodwill of the Business in which they are used, and shall, within ten (10) Business Days after the Effective Date: (a) transfer to the Company (or to an account controlled by the Company) every domain name, social-media handle, app-store developer account and similar registration held by the Assignor for the Business; and (b) execute such forms as are required for the Company to apply to record the assignment of any registered or applied-for trade mark with the Registrar of Trade Marks under the Trade Marks Act, 1999.

3.8 **Know-how and trade secrets.** To the extent that Know-how and trade secrets are not capable of assignment as property under Applicable Law, the Assignor: (a) agrees that, as between the Parties, the Company is exclusively entitled to use, disclose and exploit them; (b) shall disclose them fully to the Company; and (c) shall keep them confidential in accordance with Clause 9 and not use them for any purpose other than the Business of the Company.

3.9 **Ratification.** The Assignor ratifies and confirms every use, exploitation, registration and application in respect of any Assigned IP made by or on behalf of the Company before the Effective Date, and confirms that any assignment of Assigned IP made under any earlier agreement between the Assignor and the Company remains effective. To the extent any earlier assignment was ineffective or incomplete for any reason (including non-compliance with section 19 of the Copyright Act, 1957), this Agreement operates as an assignment of the relevant rights with effect from the Effective Date and, to the fullest extent permitted by Applicable Law, as a confirmation that the Company has held those rights beneficially from the date of their creation.

3.10 **Rights incapable of assignment.** If and to the extent that any Assigned IP is not capable of assignment under Applicable Law, the Assignor grants to the Company an exclusive (including as against the Assignor), perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free and fully paid-up licence, transferable and sub-licensable through multiple tiers, to use, exploit and enforce that Assigned IP in any manner, and the Assignor irrevocably undertakes not to exercise or enforce any such right against the Company, its Affiliates, successors, licensees or customers.

3.11 **Bare trust.** Pending completion of any formality required to vest any Assigned IP in the Company, the Assignor holds that Assigned IP and all benefits arising from it on trust for the Company absolutely.

> **Drafting note:** For a pre-incorporation founder, section 17 of the Copyright Act, 1957 (employer as first owner of works made by employees under a contract of service) cannot apply, because there was no employer when the work was created; an express written assignment such as this one is the only route to title. Even for current employees, section 17 covers only works made in the course of employment and does not extend to patents or inventions, for which there is no statutory employer-ownership rule in India; an express assignment is therefore advisable for all personnel. Contractors and advisors are never covered by section 17. Clause 3.5 is drafted to satisfy section 68 of the Patents Act, 1970, under which an assignment of a patent or a share in it is not valid unless it is in writing and the agreement is reduced to the form of a document embodying all its terms and conditions, duly executed. After execution, the Company should apply to register its title to any granted patent with the Controller under section 69 of the Patents Act, 1970 [verify the current form number and fee in the Patents Rules, 2003], to record the assignment of registered designs under the Designs Act, 2000 [verify the applicable section and form], and to record assignments of trade marks under section 45 of the Trade Marks Act, 1999; unregistered assignments may not be admitted as evidence of title until registered.

## 4. Excluded Inventions

4.1 The Assignor has listed in Schedule 2 all inventions, works and other subject matter that the Assignor owns (alone or jointly with others) as at the commencement of the Engagement, that relate to the Business, and that the Assignor wishes to exclude from this Agreement. If Schedule 2 is left blank or states "None", the Assignor represents that there are no Excluded Inventions.

4.2 The Assignor shall not incorporate, or permit to be incorporated, any Excluded Invention or any third-party intellectual property into any product, service, process or work of the Company without the Company's prior written consent. If, with or without such consent, any Excluded Invention is incorporated into, or is necessary for the use of, any product, service, process or work of the Company, the Assignor grants to the Company a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free and fully paid-up licence, transferable and sub-licensable through multiple tiers, to make, have made, use, sell, import, reproduce, modify, prepare derivative works of, distribute, communicate to the public and otherwise exploit that Excluded Invention as part of, or in connection with, that product, service, process or work.

## 5. Moral Rights

5.1 To the fullest extent permitted by Applicable Law, the Assignor irrevocably and unconditionally waives, and undertakes not to assert or exercise against the Company, its Affiliates, successors in title, licensees, customers or assigns, any right under section 57 of the Copyright Act, 1957 or any equivalent right in any jurisdiction (including the right to claim authorship of an Assigned Work and the right to restrain, or claim damages in respect of, any distortion, mutilation, modification or other act in relation to an Assigned Work), including in respect of: (a) any adaptation, translation, modification, combination or deletion of any Assigned Work; (b) any use of an Assigned Work without attribution, or with attribution to the Company; and (c) any use of an Assigned Work in conjunction with other works.

5.2 To the extent such rights cannot be waived under Applicable Law, the Assignor consents to each of the acts described in Clause 5.1 and agrees that none of them shall be regarded as prejudicial to the Assignor's honour or reputation.

> **Drafting note:** Section 57 of the Copyright Act, 1957 gives the author special rights (to claim authorship, and to restrain or claim damages for distortion, mutilation or modification prejudicial to honour or reputation) that exist independently of the author's copyright and even after its assignment. Indian courts have treated these rights as significant (see Amar Nath Sehgal v. Union of India (Delhi High Court, 2005)), and whether they can be waived outright is unsettled. Clause 5 therefore combines a waiver, an undertaking not to assert and a consent; this is the conventional approach but does not guarantee that a court would refuse relief to the author in an extreme case.

## 6. Disclosure, Records and Delivery

6.1 The Assignor shall, within ten (10) Business Days after the Effective Date, and thereafter promptly after creating any further Assigned IP: (a) disclose to the Company in writing full details of all Assigned IP that is not already known to the Company; and (b) deliver to the Company all Records in the Assignor's possession or control, including by transferring ownership of, or granting the Company administrator access to, all source code repositories, cloud accounts, design-tool workspaces and other platforms on which Records are held, and by providing all passwords, credentials and access keys.

6.2 After delivering the Records, the Assignor shall not retain any copy of them except with the Company's written consent or to the extent required for the Assignor's continuing Engagement, and shall on the Company's request delete any copy retained on personal devices or accounts and confirm deletion in writing.

6.3 The Assignor shall, on request, provide the Company with a written statement of all open-source software, third-party materials, datasets and artificial intelligence tools used in creating any Assigned Work that is software or data, identifying the applicable licence or terms of use.

## 7. Further Assurance and Power of Attorney

7.1 The Assignor shall, at the Company's request and expense (save for the Assignor's own time, which is covered by the Consideration), both during the Engagement and at any time after it ends, promptly: (a) execute all documents, including confirmatory deeds of assignment, declarations, statements of inventorship and assignments in the form required by any patent office, designs office, trade marks registry, copyright office or domain registrar in any country; (b) give evidence and testimony; and (c) do all other acts and things, in each case as may reasonably be required to vest the Assigned IP in the Company, to obtain, record, maintain, defend and enforce registrations and other protection for the Assigned IP in any country, and to give full effect to this Agreement.

7.2 The Assignor shall, if the Company so requests, execute a power of attorney in favour of the Company substantially in the form set out in Schedule 3, appointing the Company and any of its directors as the Assignor's attorney to execute documents and do acts in the Assignor's name for the purposes of Clause 7.1 if the Assignor fails to do so within ten (10) Business Days after a written request. The Assignor acknowledges that such power of attorney is given to secure the Company's interest in the Assigned IP, and is intended to be irrevocable as an agency coupled with an interest within the meaning of section 202 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.

> **Drafting note:** A power of attorney is listed in the First Schedule to the Information Technology Act, 2000, so it cannot be validly executed by electronic signature; it must be signed in wet ink. It must also be stamped under the applicable State stamp law (powers of attorney have their own article, with duty depending on the powers conferred and whether consideration is involved [verify the article and duty for the relevant State]) and is usually notarised (and, if executed outside India, notarised or apostilled and stamped within the prescribed period after it is received in India [verify]). That is why the power of attorney is in a separate Schedule to be executed as its own instrument, rather than embedded in this Agreement, which may otherwise be e-signed. Whether a power of attorney survives the death or incapacity of the donor depends on whether it is coupled with an interest (section 202 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872); a court may scrutinise that claim, so do not rely on the power of attorney as the only means of perfecting title.

## 8. Representations and Warranties

8.1 The Assignor represents and warrants to the Company, as at the Effective Date, that:

(a) the Assignor is the sole author, creator and inventor of the Assigned IP (save to the extent disclosed in Schedule 1 as created jointly with other named individuals, each of whom has assigned or will assign their rights to the Company), and is the sole legal and beneficial owner of all of the Assignor's rights in it;

(b) the Assigned IP is original to the Assignor and, to the best of the Assignor's knowledge after reasonable enquiry, its use and exploitation by the Company does not and will not infringe or misappropriate the Intellectual Property Rights or other rights of any third party;

(c) the Assignor has not assigned, licensed, charged or otherwise encumbered, or agreed to assign, license, charge or encumber, any of the Assigned IP to or in favour of any person other than the Company;

(d) none of the Assigned IP was created in the course of the Assignor's employment by, or engagement with, any person other than the Company (including any former employer, university, research institute or government body), or using the facilities, funding or confidential information of any such person, and no such person has any claim to it, save as disclosed in Schedule 1;

(e) the Assignor has not used in the Assigned IP any source code, data, design or other material obtained in breach of any obligation of confidence, licence or Applicable Law;

(f) the Assignor has disclosed to the Company all open-source software and third-party materials incorporated in any Assigned Work that is software, together with the applicable licences;

(g) the Assignor has disclosed to the Company any material portion of any Assigned Work that was generated using an artificial intelligence tool;

(h) there is no pending or, to the Assignor's knowledge, threatened claim, proceeding or dispute relating to any of the Assigned IP; and

(i) the Assignor has full capacity to enter into and perform this Agreement, and doing so does not breach any agreement or obligation binding on the Assignor.

8.2 The Assignor shall promptly notify the Company in writing if the Assignor becomes aware of any fact or matter that makes, or may make, any of the representations in Clause 8.1 untrue or misleading, or of any actual or suspected infringement of any Assigned IP by a third party.

> **Drafting note:** For an employee or junior contractor, consider qualifying the warranties in Clause 8.1(b) and (d) by the Assignor's knowledge and limiting the indemnity in Clause 10 to an amount the individual can realistically bear, to keep the document fair and enforceable. Investors usually require unqualified warranties from founders.

## 9. Confidentiality

9.1 The Assignor shall keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential, shall not use it for any purpose other than performing the Engagement for the Company, and shall not disclose it to any person without the Company's prior written consent, save as required by Applicable Law (in which case the Assignor shall, to the extent lawful, give the Company prompt prior notice). The Assignor shall not input any Confidential Information into any artificial intelligence tool that retains inputs or uses them for training without the Company's prior written consent.

9.2 The obligations in Clause 9.1 continue without limit of time for so long as the relevant information remains confidential, and are in addition to any confidentiality obligations of the Assignor under any other agreement with the Company.

## 10. Indemnity

10.1 The Assignor shall indemnify the Company against all losses, liabilities, damages, costs (including reasonable legal fees) and expenses suffered or incurred by the Company arising out of any breach of the representations and warranties in Clause 8.1, or any claim by a third party that is inconsistent with them.

10.2 Subject to Applicable Law, the aggregate liability of the Assignor under Clause 10.1 shall not exceed {{assignor_liability_cap}}, save in the case of fraud or wilful misrepresentation by the Assignor.
