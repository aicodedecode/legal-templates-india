# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Category:** Startup Core · **Type:** agreement · **Jurisdiction:** India · **Status:** Draft

## Overview

A two-way confidentiality agreement for two businesses that will each share confidential information while they evaluate or negotiate a relationship: a partnership, an integration, a joint pitch, a commercial contract or an acquisition. Both parties take on the same obligations as discloser and recipient. The template is written for Indian law, with arbitration seated in India, and covers points that many older Indian NDAs leave out: restrictions on feeding confidential information into AI tools, personal data under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and current Supreme Court law on stamping and arbitrator appointment.

## When to use it

- Exploratory discussions where both sides will disclose meaningful information (product roadmaps, pricing, technical architecture, customer data).
- Before a joint venture, reseller, integration or co-development discussion.
- Before negotiating a definitive commercial agreement, where the definitive agreement will later replace this NDA's terms for new disclosures.
- Merger or acquisition talks at an early stage, before a more detailed deal-specific confidentiality agreement.

## When not to use it

- Only your company will disclose. Use the **One-Way NDA**, which is more protective of the discloser.
- The other side will create work for you. Use the **Consultancy Agreement**, which also assigns IP.
- Ongoing processing of personal data on one party's behalf. Add a **Data Processing Agreement**.
- Institutional investors at the pitch stage. Most venture funds will not sign NDAs; share only what you would be comfortable seeing in the market.
- Listed-company transactions involving unpublished price sensitive information. Use a deal-specific agreement that deals fully with the SEBI insider trading regulations.

## Key provisions

- **Broad definition of Confidential Information** (cl. 1.1(e)). It covers oral and unmarked information, the fact that discussions are happening, and anything derived from the information, including AI tool outputs.
- **Permitted Purpose and need-to-know disclosure** (cl. 2). Each party is liable for its Representatives.
- **AI and machine-learning restrictions** (cl. 3). The recipient may not input confidential information into third-party or training AI tools, may not train or fine-tune models on it, and may not create embeddings from it. Enterprise deployments with no-retention, no-training terms are permitted; delete cl. 3.2 for an outright ban.
- **Standard exclusions** (cl. 4), with an anti-combination rule. Personal data stays protected even where an exclusion applies.
- **Compelled disclosure** (cl. 5), with notice and narrowing obligations.
- **Personal data** (cl. 6). Lawful-basis warranty, security safeguards and a 24-hour breach notice.
- **Return or destruction** (cl. 7), with a carve-out for legally required retention and automatic back-ups.
- **No licence, no warranty, no obligation to proceed** (cl. 8).
- **Narrow employee non-solicit** (cl. 9).
- **Term and survival** (cl. 10). A disclosure period, a survival period, and indefinite protection for trade secrets while they remain secret.
- **Remedies** (cl. 12). Specific performance, injunctions and interim measures, with no liquidated-damages figure.
- **Arbitration** (cl. 13). Seat and venue are stated separately, the arbitrator is appointed jointly or under s.11, and pre-institution mediation applies where a dispute goes to court.

## India-specific notes

- **Stamp duty.** Stamp duty is payable under the stamp law of the State where the agreement is executed. NDAs are usually stamped under the residuary "agreement" article, which carries a modest fixed duty in most States; check the article and rate for your State. Stamp the agreement before or at signing, ideally by e-stamping. Under *In re: Interplay between Arbitration Agreements and the Indian Stamp Act* (Supreme Court, seven judges, 2023), an unstamped agreement is not void, but it cannot be admitted in evidence or acted on until the duty and penalty are paid. If the agreement is executed in one State and enforced in another, differential duty may be payable.
- **Restraint of trade.** Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 voids restraints of trade. This NDA has no non-compete. Indian courts have taken different views on whether non-solicitation covenants are enforceable after the agreement ends.
- **Remedies.** Since the 2018 amendment to the Specific Relief Act, 1963, specific performance is the general rule. Because the NDA can be terminated on notice, the more reliable remedy is an injunction enforcing the negative covenants, together with interim relief under s.9 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996. Section 74 of the Contract Act limits any liquidated sum to reasonable compensation.
- **Arbitration.** Clauses that let one party appoint the arbitrator alone are invalid (*Perkins Eastman*, 2019; *CORE*, Constitution Bench, 2024). State the seat expressly.
- **E-signature and evidence.** E-signing is valid under ss.3A and 10A of the Information Technology Act, 2000. Keep the signing platform's audit trail: proving an electronic record requires a certificate under s.63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.
- **Data protection.** The DPDP Act and Rules come into force in phases, so check which obligations apply on the date you sign. Cyber incidents may also need to be reported to CERT-In within six hours.

## Customisation checklist

- [ ] Describe the Purpose narrowly but accurately.
- [ ] Set the disclosure period and the survival period. Two to three years for each is common; trade secrets survive regardless.
- [ ] Decide whether to keep the enterprise-AI permission in cl. 3.2.
- [ ] Decide whether to keep the non-solicit in cl. 9, and for how long.
- [ ] Choose the arbitration seat and venue, and decide whether to use an institution (MCIA, DIAC, IIAC).
- [ ] Decide who pays stamp duty, and confirm the article and rate for the State of execution.
- [ ] Add a SEBI insider-trading acknowledgement if a listed company is involved.
- [ ] Confirm that each signatory has authority to sign, by board resolution or power of attorney.
