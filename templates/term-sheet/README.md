# Term Sheet for Seed / Series A Investment (CCPS)

**Category:** Fundraising & Corporate · **Type:** term-sheet · **Jurisdiction:** India · **Status:** Draft

## Overview

A term sheet for a priced seed or Series A round in an Indian private limited company. Investors subscribe to compulsorily convertible preference shares (CCPS), which are the market-standard instrument for venture investment in India.

It sets out the commercial deal:
- valuation, round size and the ESOP Pool
- FEMA pricing and Press Note 3 compliance
- anti-dilution on a broad-based weighted average basis
- a 1x non-participating liquidation preference
- board composition and quorum, and Reserved Matters (Schedule 1)
- information rights, founder commitments and vesting
- ROFR, tag-along, exit and drag-along
- representations and warranties, and indemnity
- a CP and Closing Checklist (Schedule 2): Item | Owner | Form & deadline | Status

Only four provisions bind the parties: **confidentiality, exclusivity, costs, and governing law and dispute resolution** (paragraph 29). Defined terms match the shareholders' agreement and iSAFE templates in this repository.

## When to use it

- A lead investor has agreed headline terms and you want them in writing before lawyers draft the share subscription agreement (SSA) and shareholders' agreement (SHA).
- The company is an Indian private limited company issuing CCPS for cash, to resident investors, non-resident investors or both.
- You want a balanced starting point that a founder can sign without giving away unusual rights.

## When not to use it

- **Pre-seed or bridge rounds without a priced valuation.** Use the iSAFE (CCPS with a valuation cap and discount), or a convertible note if the company is DPIIT-recognised.
- **LLPs, or companies being acquired.** Acquisitions need a share purchase term sheet with different protections.
- **Rounds in a foreign holding company** (for example, a Delaware or Singapore "flip" structure). Those follow foreign law.
- **Debt or venture debt.**

## Key provisions

- **Definitions (para 1):** a shared glossary used across this repository's term sheet, iSAFE and SHA, so that terms carry over to the transaction documents unchanged.
- **Investment and pricing (paras 3–5):**
  - The pre-money valuation is on a Fully Diluted Basis and includes the expanded ESOP Pool.
  - The CCPS carry a nominal non-cumulative dividend, convert 1:1 (subject to adjustment), and convert mandatorily before a Qualified IPO or at a long-stop date.
  - As-converted voting is contractual (s.47).
- **Regulatory (para 4):**
  - Press Note 3 declaration and government approval for investors from countries sharing a land border with India.
  - FEMA fair value certified by a chartered accountant or a SEBI-registered merchant banker.
  - Registered valuer's report under the Act.
  - Allotment within 60 days or refund; FC-GPR within 30 days.
  - Notes that angel tax was abolished from AY 2025-26.
- **Investor protections (paras 6–8):** pre-emption, broad-based weighted average anti-dilution with Exempted Issuances, and a 1x non-participating liquidation preference with deemed Liquidation Events.
- **Governance (paras 9–11):**
  - Investor Director quorum rights, with an adjourned-meeting fall-back.
  - Reserved Matters, with deemed consent for non-fundamental items.
  - Information rights.
- **Founders (para 12):**
  - Non-compete only during tenure; non-solicit afterwards.
  - IP assignment and lock-in.
  - Reverse vesting with good leaver and bad leaver outcomes.
- **Transfers and exit (paras 13–14):** ROFR and tag-along, and best-efforts exit within the Exit Period. The drag-along is conditional on a Founder right of first offer, a valuation floor, and limited warranties. There is no put option, because of NDI Rules optionality limits.
- **Articles (para 18):** restated Articles adopted by special resolution, with MGT-14 filed within 30 days.
- **Binding provisions (paras 19–22, 29):**
  - Costs, exclusivity, confidentiality, and governing law and dispute resolution.
  - The arbitration clause states the seat and the venue separately.
  - The arbitrator is appointed mutually or by an institution, never unilaterally.

## India-specific notes

- **Companies Act, 2013:**
  - The CCPS are issued by private placement under section 42 read with section 62(1)(c).
  - Steps: a special resolution of shareholders, an offer letter in Form PAS-4, a record of offers in PAS-5, application money held in a separate bank account, and a return of allotment in Form PAS-3.
  - A registered valuer's report is needed for a preferential issue.
  - Verify current timelines with your company secretary.
- **FEMA / NDI Rules (non-resident investors):**
  - Check the sector's entry route and cap. If the investor's beneficial owner is in a country sharing a land border with India, government approval may be needed.
  - The issue price must be at least fair value, and the conversion formula must be fixed when the CCPS are issued.
  - Report the issue in Form FC-GPR.
  - Put options and assured returns are restricted.
  - The template puts these in drafting notes rather than hard-coding numbers.
- **Press Note 3 (2020):** an investor, or a beneficial owner of its investment, from a country sharing a land border with India needs government approval. Check this early, because approval can take months.
- **Stamp duty:**
  - *On the term sheet:* duty depends on the State where it is signed or first brought into that State. Many parties stamp it as an agreement because the binding clauses are enforceable. E-stamp it before or at signing. An unstamped instrument is not void, but it cannot be admitted in evidence until the duty and penalty are paid (*In re Interplay*, Supreme Court, seven-judge bench, 2023).
  - *On the share issue:* since the 2020 amendments to the Indian Stamp Act, 1899, duty on issuing shares is levied centrally at a low ad valorem rate. It is collected through the depository for dematerialised shares, or otherwise paid in the State of the registered office.
  - Verify the current rate and mechanism before closing.
- **Dematerialisation:** private companies that are not "small companies" must issue securities only in demat form and must have dematerialised their existing holdings. Check the company's status and whether it has an ISIN before the round.
- **Tax:** the levy on share premium in excess of fair market value ("angel tax") was abolished with effect from AY 2025-26 by the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024. The Income-tax Act, 2025 now applies, so confirm any remaining valuation requirements with a tax adviser. The template deliberately cites no sections.
- **Arbitration:** the clause states the seat and the venue separately, and requires mutual or institutional appointment. A unilateral appointment clause is invalid (*Perkins Eastman*, 2019; *CORE*, Constitution Bench, 2024).
- **Enforceability:** the non-binding paragraphs give no right to sue for the investment. Exclusivity and confidentiality are enforceable contracts, but damages for breach of exclusivity are hard to prove. Founder non-competes after exit are generally void under section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872.
- **E-signature:** permitted under the Information Technology Act, 2000.

## Customisation checklist

- [ ] Pre-money valuation, round size, and how the round is split among investors
- [ ] ESOP pool size, and whether it is counted in the pre-money or post-money valuation
- [ ] Dividend rate and the long-stop date for conversion (keep it within the statutory limit on preference share tenure)
- [ ] Board size, investor director and observer rights, and the quorum fall-back
- [ ] Reserved matters: delete operational items and add monetary thresholds
- [ ] Shareholding threshold for keeping investor rights
- [ ] Founder vesting percentage, period, acceleration trigger and lock-in
- [ ] Exit period and drag-along timing and conditions
- [ ] Cap on reimbursed investor costs, and the exclusivity period
- [ ] FEMA checks for each non-resident investor (route, cap, pricing, land-border rule)
- [ ] Arbitration: seat, venue, and mutual (Option A) or institutional (Option B) appointment
- [ ] Drag-along valuation floor and the debt threshold for Reserved Matters
- [ ] Good leaver and bad leaver definitions, and credit for founder service before Closing
- [ ] Owners and dates filled in on the CP and Closing Checklist (Schedule 2)
- [ ] Cap table reconciled to the register of members and the latest PAS-3 (attach it to the SSA)
