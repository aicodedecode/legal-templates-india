**Subject: Offer of employment for the position of {{job_title}}**

Following our recent discussions, we are pleased to offer you employment with {{company_name}} (the **"Company"**) on the terms set out in this letter (this **"Offer Letter"**). This Offer Letter is a summary of the principal terms. Your employment will be governed in detail by the Company's employment agreement (the **"Employment Agreement"**), which you will be required to sign on or before your joining date, and by the Company's policies as in force from time to time, including the Employee Handbook and the Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Policy.

## 1. Position and Reporting

1.1 You will be employed in the position of **{{job_title}}** in the {{department}} team and will report to the {{reporting_manager}} or such other person as the Company may designate.

1.2 Your role is primarily managerial, administrative or of a professional nature, and your precise duties will be set out in the Employment Agreement and your role description.

> **Drafting note:** If the role is that of a "worker" under the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (broadly, anyone other than those employed mainly in a managerial or administrative capacity, or in a supervisory capacity drawing wages above the notified threshold), additional statutory protections apply: standing orders, retrenchment conditions, and the jurisdiction of labour forums. Do not describe a role as managerial if it is not. Courts look at the substance of the duties, not the title.

## 2. Place of Work

2.1 Your principal place of work will be {{work_location}}. The Company may require you to travel, or to work from another office or location in India, in accordance with its policies and the Employment Agreement.

## 3. Joining Date

3.1 Your expected date of joining is **{{joining_date}}** (the **"Joining Date"**). If you do not join on the Joining Date, or on any later date agreed in writing by the Company, this offer will automatically lapse unless the Company agrees otherwise in writing.

## 4. Remuneration

4.1 Your total annual cost to company (**"CTC"**) will be **{{annual_ctc}}**, structured as set out in the table below. Fixed pay is paid monthly in arrears, on or before the last working day of each month (and in any event within the time required by the Code on Wages, 2019), after deduction of tax at source and other statutory deductions.

| Component | Monthly (₹) | Annual (₹) |
|---|---|---|
| Basic pay | {{basic_monthly}} | {{basic_annual}} |
| House rent allowance | {{hra_monthly}} | {{hra_annual}} |
| Special allowance | {{special_allowance_monthly}} | {{special_allowance_annual}} |
| **Gross fixed pay (A)** | **{{fixed_gross_monthly}}** | **{{fixed_gross_annual}}** |
| Employer contribution to provident fund | {{employer_pf_monthly}} | {{employer_pf_annual}} |
| Gratuity (provision; payable as per law) | – | {{gratuity_annual}} |
| Group health insurance premium | – | {{insurance_annual}} |
| **Retirement and other benefits (B)** | | |
| Target variable pay (C) | – | {{variable_pay_annual}} |
| **Total CTC (A + B + C)** | | **{{annual_ctc}}** |

4.2 **Wages.** For the purposes of the Labour Codes, your "wages" comprise your basic pay and any dearness allowance or retaining allowance. Where the components excluded from "wages" by law (such as house rent allowance, conveyance, employer contributions to provident fund and pension, gratuity and variable pay) together exceed 50% of your total remuneration, or such other proportion as is notified, the excess will be treated as wages for computing statutory benefits, including gratuity and provident fund where applicable.

> **Drafting note:** Under the Code on Wages, 2019, "wages" excludes specified components (including HRA, conveyance, statutory bonus, employer PF/pension contributions, overtime, commission and gratuity), but if the excluded components exceed one half (or the notified proportion) of all remuneration, the excess is added back to wages. Structure basic pay plus dearness allowance at not less than 50% of total remuneration to avoid surprises in PF, gratuity and leave encashment calculations, and verify the current rules with your payroll provider. Employer contributions, gratuity and insurance premiums are costs to the Company, not cash in hand; say so clearly to avoid disputes.

4.3 **Variable pay.** Your target variable pay is payable subject to your performance and the Company's performance against goals set by the Company, and to the terms of the Company's variable pay policy. It is not guaranteed and is payable only if you are in employment and not serving notice on the payment date, unless the policy says otherwise.

4.4 **Statutory benefits.** You will be covered, as applicable, by provident fund, employees' state insurance and gratuity under the Code on Social Security, 2020, and by the statutory bonus provisions of the Code on Wages, 2019 if your wages fall within the eligibility threshold.

4.5 **Review.** Your remuneration will be reviewed annually in line with Company policy. Any increase is at the Company's discretion.

4.6 **Tax.** All payments are subject to deduction of tax at source and other deductions required by applicable tax laws, including the Income-tax Act, 2025. You are responsible for any tax payable on your remuneration over and above the tax deducted at source.

## 5. Probation

5.1 You will be on probation for {{probation_months}} months from the Joining Date. The Company may extend the probation period once, by up to the same period, by written notice. Your employment will be confirmed in writing on successful completion of probation.

5.2 During probation, either party may end your employment by giving {{probation_notice_days}} days' written notice or pay in lieu of notice. After confirmation, the notice period is {{notice_period_days}} days, as set out in the Employment Agreement.

## 6. Employee Stock Options

6.1 Subject to approval by the Company's Board of Directors (or its nomination and remuneration committee) and to the terms of the Company's Employee Stock Option Plan as in force from time to time, management will recommend that you be granted {{esop_indicative_options}} options to acquire equity shares of the Company. The number, exercise price, vesting schedule and other terms of any grant will be set out only in a grant letter issued under the Plan, and nothing in this Offer Letter creates any right to options until a grant letter is issued.

> **Drafting note:** Do not promise a percentage of the company, a specific exercise price or a vesting start date in the offer letter. Grants under s.62(1)(b) of the Companies Act, 2013 can only be made in accordance with a plan approved by special resolution, and the minimum vesting period between grant and vesting is one year under the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014. Delete this section if the role is not ESOP-eligible.

## 7. Conditions of this Offer

7.1 This offer, and your continued employment, are conditional on:

(a) satisfactory completion of background verification, which may include verification of your identity, address, educational qualifications, previous employment, references and (where permitted by law and relevant to the role) criminal records;

(b) your submitting, on or before the Joining Date, the documents listed in Annexure A;

(c) your being legally entitled to work in India and, where applicable, holding a valid visa and registration;

(d) you not being subject to any agreement, undertaking or court order that would prevent you from joining the Company or performing your role; and

(e) your signing the Employment Agreement and the Company's confidentiality and invention assignment documentation on or before the Joining Date.

7.2 If any of these conditions is not met, or if any information you have provided is found to be false or misleading, the Company may withdraw this offer or, if you have joined, terminate your employment in accordance with the Employment Agreement.

7.3 **Your personal data.** By accepting this offer you acknowledge that the Company will process your personal data, including for background verification by third-party agencies acting as its Data Processors, for the purposes of recruitment and employment, as described in the Company's employee privacy notice. The Company relies on the legitimate uses permitted for employment purposes under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where required, on your consent, which you may withdraw at any time without affecting processing already carried out. You may contact {{company_email}} to exercise your rights as a Data Principal or to raise a grievance, and you may thereafter approach the Data Protection Board of India.

> **Drafting note:** The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 are being brought into force in phases; check which obligations apply on the date of the offer [verify]. Processing for the purposes of employment is a legitimate use under s.7 of the Act (clause (i)) [verify], so consent is not always required, but giving candidates a clear notice is good practice and is expected by most employee privacy programmes. Background checks that go beyond what is necessary for employment (for example, social media screening) may need separate, specific consent and a privacy notice.

## 8. Prior Obligations and Confidentiality

8.1 You confirm that you will not bring to the Company, or use in your work, any confidential information or intellectual property belonging to a previous employer or any other person, and that you have disclosed to the Company any restrictive covenants that bind you.

8.2 The terms of this Offer Letter are confidential and should not be disclosed to any person other than your immediate family and professional advisers.

## 9. Other Terms

9.1 Working hours, leave, holidays, notice, termination, confidentiality, intellectual property, non-solicitation and other terms of your employment will be as set out in the Employment Agreement, the Employee Handbook and the applicable Shops and Establishments Act of the State in which you work.

9.2 This Offer Letter is governed by the laws of India. It does not create any binding obligation of employment until accepted by you in accordance with Section 10 and the conditions in Section 7 are satisfied.

9.3 This Offer Letter may be signed and accepted electronically, including by an electronic signature or electronic authentication technique recognised under the Information Technology Act, 2000. The electronic record of your acceptance, together with the audit trail of the signing platform, may be relied on in evidence in accordance with section 63 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.

9.4 Any dispute arising out of this Offer Letter before the Employment Agreement is signed shall be resolved in the manner provided in the Employment Agreement, and, until then, the courts at the place of your principal place of work shall have jurisdiction.

9.5 If there is any inconsistency between this Offer Letter and the Employment Agreement, the Employment Agreement shall prevail.

> **Drafting note:** An offer letter is generally treated as an agreement for stamp duty purposes in some States and as exempt or not chargeable in others; check the position under the Stamp Act of the State where it is accepted. Most employers do not stamp offer letters but do stamp the Employment Agreement. An unstamped or under-stamped instrument is not void but is inadmissible in evidence until the duty and penalty are paid (In re Interplay, Supreme Court seven-judge bench, 2023), so if you intend to rely on the offer letter itself (for example, for a joining bonus clawback), stamp it.

## Annexure A – Documents to be Submitted on Joining

1. Copies of educational certificates and mark sheets (highest qualification and graduation).
2. Relieving letter and last three salary slips from your most recent employer (or proof of resignation acceptance if you are serving notice).
3. Proof of identity and address (any one officially valid document; you may mask the Aadhaar number).
4. PAN card.
5. Universal Account Number (UAN) for provident fund, if you have one.
6. Bank account details for salary credit.
7. Two recent passport-size photographs.
8. Signed background verification authorisation form.
9. Nomination forms for provident fund, gratuity and group insurance.
10. For foreign nationals: passport, valid employment visa and foreigner registration details, as applicable.

> **Drafting note:** Do not make Aadhaar mandatory for identity proof; the Supreme Court in the Puttaswamy (Aadhaar) judgment (2018) restricted mandatory Aadhaar authentication by private entities. Accept any officially valid document, and ask candidates to mask the first eight digits if they choose to provide Aadhaar.
