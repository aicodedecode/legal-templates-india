# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) Policy

**Category:** HR Policies · **Type:** policy · **Jurisdiction:** India · **Status:** Draft

## Overview

A complete workplace sexual harassment policy under the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (the "POSH Act") and the 2013 Rules. It covers:

- **Scope and definitions:** the statutory definitions of sexual harassment, employee and workplace, extended by policy to remote work and digital channels, with modern illustrations including AI-generated intimate content.
- **Internal Committee:** constitution, term, removal, conflicts and quorum.
- **Employer duties.**
- **Complaints:** the 3-month time limit (extendable by 3 months) and the SHe-Box route.
- **Conciliation:** no monetary settlement.
- **Inquiry:** natural justice, no lawyers, the ex parte rule and the 90-day limit.
- **Interim relief.**
- **Outcomes:** the report within 10 days, employer action within 60 days, compensation factors, and false or malicious complaints.
- **Confidentiality, appeal and retaliation:** confidentiality under section 16, the 90-day appeal, and protection against retaliation.
- **Reporting:** the annual report to the District Officer.
- **Other routes and penalties:** the Local Committee and penalties for non-compliance.

It ends with a complaint form, an Internal Committee constitution table and an employee acknowledgement.

## When to use it

- Any employer in India with **10 or more workers** at an office or administrative unit. Such an employer must constitute an Internal Committee (section 4) and should adopt a written policy.
- Smaller employers that want a policy and a voluntary Internal Committee, or that need to explain the Local Committee route to staff.
- During fundraising due diligence, when investors routinely ask for the POSH policy, the Internal Committee order and the annual reports.

## When not to use it

- **As the only anti-harassment document.** General harassment, bullying and discrimination that is not sexual in nature belongs in the code of conduct in the employee handbook.
- **Government establishments and educational institutions.** These may be governed by specific service rules or UGC regulations that change the procedure.
- **Outside India.** Foreign offices need a policy under their local law.

## Key provisions

- **Gender-neutral by policy (para 3.3):** the Act protects only women. The policy accepts complaints from all genders, but states clearly that statutory remedies (the section 18 appeal, the Local Committee, and District Officer recovery) may not apply, and provides an internal review instead.
- **Workplace (para 4.1):** section 2(o), including transport and places visited, extended by policy to home working and virtual workspaces.
- **Internal Committee (para 5):**
  - a Presiding Officer who is a senior woman employee;
  - at least 2 employee members;
  - 1 external member from an NGO or association, or a person familiar with the issues;
  - at least half the members women;
  - a term of up to 3 years;
  - removal grounds under section 4(5);
  - a quorum of 3 including the Presiding Officer (Rule 7(7)).
- **Procedure (paras 7–11):**
  - a copy of the complaint goes to the Respondent within 7 working days, and the reply is due within 10 working days;
  - no legal practitioners;
  - an ex parte decision is possible after 3 missed hearings, with 15 days' notice;
  - the inquiry must finish within 90 days and the report within 10 days;
  - the employer must act within 60 days.
- **False complaints (para 13):** action only where malice is established after an inquiry. A mere inability to prove the complaint is not malice.
- **Confidentiality (para 14):** sections 16–17, with DPDP-aligned record handling.

## India-specific notes

- **Statute and Rules:** POSH Act, 2013 and POSH Rules, 2013. The Supreme Court in *Aureliano Fernandes v. State of Goa* (2023) required strict compliance and public display of Internal Committee details.
- **Criminal law:** offences now sit in the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. Section 11 of the Act still refers to the IPC, so map it to the BNS [verify].
- **Labour Codes:** the four Labour Codes are in force. Appeals under Rule 11 refer to the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, now subsumed in the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 [verify]. Add sexual harassment as misconduct in your standing orders.
- **Company law disclosure:** the Board's report must state compliance with the Internal Committee requirement. Recent amendments to the Companies (Accounts) Rules, 2014 require complaint statistics [verify].
- **Penalties (s.26):**
  - a fine of up to ₹ 50,000;
  - double the punishment on a repeat contravention;
  - possible cancellation of the licence or registration [verify any amendment].
  - Breach of confidentiality under section 17 and Rule 12 carries a separate penalty [verify the amount].
- **SHe-Box:** the Ministry of Women and Child Development's online complaint portal routes complaints to the relevant Internal Committee or Local Committee. Mention it to employees.
- **Data protection:** complaint records are personal data under the DPDP Act, 2023. Restrict access and set a retention period. The DPDP Rules, 2025 take effect in phases.
- **Stamp duty and execution:** a policy is not stamped. Have it adopted by a Board resolution or a decision of the head of the organisation, and record each employee's acknowledgement, which can be given electronically.

## Customisation checklist

- [ ] Approving authority and effective date
- [ ] Offices and units covered; one Internal Committee or several (one is required for each office or unit with 10 or more workers)
- [ ] Internal Committee members appointed, the external member's eligibility checked under Rule 4, and fees agreed
- [ ] Order constituting the Internal Committee signed and displayed, in English and local languages
- [ ] Dedicated confidential email address for the Internal Committee
- [ ] Appellate authority under the service rules
- [ ] District Officer and Local Committee details for each district
- [ ] Gender-neutral extension retained or removed
- [ ] Annual awareness training and Internal Committee orientation scheduled
- [ ] Annual report calendar (submitted to the District Officer) and Board's report disclosure
- [ ] Policy referred to in offer letters, employment agreements, the handbook and vendor contracts
