# ADVISOR AGREEMENT

> **Drafting note:** This agreement is for an external advisor who gives strategic, technical, industry or fundraising guidance for a few hours a month. If the person will deliver defined work product for fees, use the Consultancy Agreement. If the person will be a director, the appointment, any remuneration and any equity must also comply with the Companies Act, 2013 (including board and shareholder approvals) and the Company's articles of association. Read the drafting notes in Clause 5 carefully before offering equity: an advisor who is not an employee or director is generally not eligible for ESOPs. Delete all drafting notes before signing.

This Advisor Agreement (this **"Agreement"**) is made on {{effective_date}} (the **"Effective Date"**)

**BETWEEN**

**{{company_name}}**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 with corporate identity number {{company_cin}}, having its registered office at {{company_address}} (the **"Company"**, which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include its successors and assigns);

**AND**

**{{advisor_name}}**, an individual residing at {{advisor_address}} (the **"Advisor"**, which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include the Advisor's legal heirs and legal representatives).

The Company and the Advisor are each a **"Party"** and together the **"Parties"**.

## Recitals

A. The Company is engaged in the business of {{company_business}}.

B. The Advisor has expertise in {{advisor_expertise}} and has agreed to advise the Company on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises in this Agreement, the Parties agree as follows:

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement:

(a) **"Advisory Work Product"** means all works, inventions, ideas, designs, materials, reports, presentations, software, data, know-how and other subject matter (whether or not protectable as Intellectual Property Rights) that the Advisor creates, conceives, develops or reduces to practice, alone or with others, in the course of providing the Services or using the Company's Confidential Information, together with all Intellectual Property Rights in them;

(b) **"AI Tool"** means any artificial intelligence or machine-learning model, system or service, including any generative AI service, that retains inputs or uses them for training or model improvement;

(c) **"Applicable Law"** means all laws, statutes, rules, regulations, notifications, directions, orders and judgments of any governmental, regulatory, judicial or quasi-judicial authority that apply to a Party or to the subject matter of this Agreement;

(d) **"Board"** means the board of directors of the Company;

(e) **"Confidential Information"** means all non-public information, in any form, relating to the Company, its Group, or their businesses, customers, users, suppliers, investors, employees, products, technology, source code, finances, plans and strategies, that the Advisor receives or creates in connection with this Agreement, including Advisory Work Product and the terms of this Agreement, but excluding information that the Advisor can show is or becomes public other than through a breach of this Agreement, was lawfully known to the Advisor free of any obligation of confidence before the Company disclosed it, or is lawfully received from a third party free of any obligation of confidence;

(f) **"Group"** means the Company and its subsidiaries from time to time;

(g) **"Intellectual Property Rights"** means copyright and related rights, patents, utility models, rights in inventions, registered and unregistered designs, trade marks, service marks, trade names and domain names, rights in get-up and goodwill and the right to sue for passing off, rights in computer software, database rights, rights in confidential information (including know-how and trade secrets), semiconductor integrated circuit layout-design rights, and all other intellectual property rights, in each case whether registered or unregistered, including all applications, renewals, extensions and revivals of them, and all similar or equivalent rights anywhere in the world;

(h) **"Services"** means the advisory services described in Clause 2 and Schedule 1; and

(i) **"Term"** has the meaning given in Clause 11.1.

1.2 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires: (a) the recitals and Schedules form part of this Agreement; (b) headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation; (c) the singular includes the plural and vice versa, and words importing a gender include every gender; (d) "include" and "including" are illustrative and do not limit the preceding words, and the ejusdem generis rule shall not apply; (e) a reference to a statute or statutory provision includes any amendment, consolidation, re-enactment or replacement of it and any subordinate legislation made under it, for the time being in force; (f) a reference to "writing" includes email but excludes instant messaging; (g) a reference to the Company's articles of association or shareholders' agreement is to that document as amended from time to time; and (h) no provision shall be construed against a Party on the ground that it prepared this Agreement.

## 2. Advisory Services

2.1 The Company engages the Advisor, and the Advisor agrees, to provide the Services described in Schedule 1 during the Term, which may include: (a) advice on strategy, product, technology, go-to-market, hiring and fundraising; (b) introductions to potential customers, partners, employees and investors; (c) attending meetings with the founders and management; and (d) such other advisory services within the Advisor's expertise as the Parties may agree from time to time.

2.2 The Advisor shall provide the Services with reasonable skill, care and diligence, in good faith and in the best interests of the Company, and in compliance with Applicable Law and the Company's reasonable policies notified to the Advisor (including policies on anti-bribery, insider information, prevention of sexual harassment at the workplace and information security).

2.3 The Advisor shall report to {{advisor_reporting_to}} or such other person as the Company may nominate.

2.4 The Advisor acts in an advisory capacity only. The Advisor has no authority to bind the Company, to make any representation or commitment on its behalf, or to hold themselves out as an employee, director, officer or agent of the Company. The Company may list the Advisor as an "advisor" on its website and in investor materials, with the Advisor's prior approval of the description used.

> **Drafting note:** If the Advisor will make paid introductions to investors, be aware that intermediaries who arrange fundraising for consideration may need to consider regulatory requirements (for example, SEBI rules on investment advisers or merchant bankers where applicable) and that success fees tied to a fundraise should be disclosed to investors. Keep fundraising support to introductions and general advice unless this has been checked.

## 3. Time Commitment

3.1 The Advisor shall make themselves available to the Company for approximately {{advisor_hours_per_month}} hours per month, including at least one scheduled meeting per month with the founders or the person named in Clause 2.3, at times and places (including by video call) mutually agreed.

3.2 The Parties acknowledge that the Advisor may have other professional commitments and that the time commitment in Clause 3.1 is a reasonable estimate rather than a guarantee of minimum hours. If the Advisor is consistently unable to meet it, either Party may raise the matter, and the Parties shall discuss in good faith adjusting the Services, the time commitment or the compensation under Clause 5.

## 4. Expenses

4.1 The Company shall reimburse the Advisor for reasonable, documented out-of-pocket expenses (including travel) incurred in providing the Services, provided that any single expense exceeding {{expense_preapproval_limit}} has been approved in writing by the Company in advance. The Advisor shall submit expense claims with supporting receipts within thirty (30) days after incurring them, and the Company shall reimburse approved claims within thirty (30) days of receipt.

## 5. Compensation

5.1 **Nature of compensation.** In consideration for the Services, the Company shall provide the Advisor with the compensation set out in this Clause 5 and Schedule 2. The Advisor is not entitled to any salary, retainer or other remuneration except as set out in Schedule 2.

> **Drafting note:** Choose ONE of the alternatives in Clause 5.2 (Alternative A, B or C) and delete the others, together with the corresponding parts of Schedule 2. Key legal constraints: (1) ESOPs under s.62(1)(b) of the Companies Act, 2013 and Rule 12 of the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014 may be granted only to "employees" as defined in that Rule, which broadly covers permanent employees working in or outside India, directors (whole-time or not, but excluding independent directors) and such employees or directors of a holding, subsidiary or associate company; an external advisor who is not an employee or director is not eligible, an independent director cannot receive stock options (s.197(7)), and promoters, members of the promoter group and directors holding (directly or indirectly) more than ten per cent of the equity are excluded, save in an eligible DPIIT-recognised startup for a limited period from incorporation [verify the current text of Rule 12(1) and the startup relaxation before relying on it]. Appointing an advisor as a non-executive director solely to make them ESOP-eligible has governance and liability consequences (directors' duties under s.166) and needs board and shareholder approvals; do not do it as a workaround. (2) Sweat equity shares under s.54 and Rule 8 are also limited to directors and employees, require a special resolution, a registered valuer's valuation of the know-how or value addition, are subject to a lock-in and to annual and overall caps (with relaxed caps for eligible startups), so they help only if the advisor is or becomes a director or employee; verify the current caps and lock-in in Rule 8 [verify]. (3) A direct issue of shares to an advisor is a preferential allotment or private placement requiring compliance with s.62(1)(c) and/or s.42 and the related rules (special resolution, valuation by a registered valuer, offer letter, return of allotment), and if the shares are issued for services rather than cash the valuation must also support the non-cash consideration. (4) Cash or phantom equity (sometimes called a cash-settled stock appreciation right) (a contractual right to a cash payment linked to the value of the Company on an exit) avoids most of these issues but is a deferred cash liability. If the Advisor is not resident in India, any issue of shares must also comply with FEMA, 1999 and the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019 (pricing guidelines, sectoral caps and reporting such as Form FC-GPR); check these before agreeing any equity. Take tax advice for both Parties, as equity received for services is generally taxable in the recipient's hands under applicable tax laws.

5.2 **Equity or equity-linked compensation.**

**[ALTERNATIVE A: ESOP grant – use only if the Advisor is eligible under the Company's ESOP Plan and Rule 12, for example because the Advisor is also a non-independent director or an employee]**

(a) Subject to approval by the Board (or the compensation committee administering the Company's employee stock option plan (the **"ESOP Plan"**)), the Company shall grant the Advisor options to acquire {{advisor_equity_amount}} equity shares of the Company under the ESOP Plan, at the exercise price and on the vesting schedule set out in Schedule 2.

(b) The options shall be governed by the ESOP Plan and the grant letter issued under it. If there is any conflict between this Agreement and the ESOP Plan, the ESOP Plan prevails. In accordance with the Companies (Share Capital and Debentures) Rules, 2014, there shall be a minimum period of one year between the grant and the vesting of options.

(c) The Advisor's eligibility under the ESOP Plan depends on the Advisor remaining an "employee" as defined in the ESOP Plan and the applicable rules. If the Advisor ceases to be eligible, the treatment of vested and unvested options is governed by the ESOP Plan.

**[ALTERNATIVE B: Direct issue of equity shares in tranches]**

(a) Subject to the Company obtaining all corporate approvals required under the Companies Act, 2013 and its articles of association (including any shareholder approval and any investor consent required under the Company's shareholders' agreement), the Company shall issue and allot to the Advisor, in tranches as each tranche vests in accordance with Schedule 2, up to an aggregate of {{advisor_equity_amount}} equity shares of the Company (the **"Advisor Shares"**).

(b) Each tranche shall be issued at the price per share, and against the consideration, determined in accordance with Schedule 2 and Applicable Law, including any valuation report required under the Companies Act, 2013 and, if the Advisor is not resident in India, the Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019.

(c) Before any issue of Advisor Shares, the Advisor shall execute a deed of adherence to the Company's shareholders' agreement (if any) in the form required by it, and the Advisor Shares shall be subject to the transfer restrictions in the Company's articles of association and that shareholders' agreement.

(d) No Advisor Shares shall be issued in respect of any tranche that has not vested on or before the date of termination of this Agreement.

> **Drafting note:** Alternative B issues shares only as each tranche vests, rather than issuing all shares up front subject to a repurchase right. This is deliberate: a company's ability to buy back its own shares from an individual is restricted under s.68 of the Companies Act, 2013, so US-style "reverse vesting" of advisor shares is hard to implement in India. If founders prefer up-front issue, the usual Indian alternative is a call option in favour of a founder or nominee over unvested shares, documented in the shareholders' agreement; take advice before using it. Each tranche issue will need its own corporate approvals and filings; batching tranches (for example, issuing annually) reduces cost.

**[ALTERNATIVE C: Phantom equity or cash compensation]**

(a) The Company grants the Advisor {{advisor_equity_amount}} phantom units (the **"Phantom Units"**), which shall vest in accordance with Schedule 2.

(b) On the occurrence of a Liquidity Event (as defined in Schedule 2) while the Advisor holds vested Phantom Units, the Company shall pay the Advisor, within thirty (30) days after the Company receives the proceeds of the Liquidity Event, a cash amount equal to the number of vested Phantom Units multiplied by the amount (if any) by which the price per equity share received or deemed received by the holders of equity shares in the Liquidity Event exceeds the base price per unit set out in Schedule 2, less applicable taxes required to be withheld.

(c) Phantom Units are a contractual right to a cash payment only. They do not confer any shares, voting rights, dividend rights or other rights of a shareholder, and are not transferable.

(d) [Where a monthly cash fee is also agreed: The Company shall pay the Advisor a fee of {{advisor_cash_fee}} per month, plus GST if applicable, against a valid invoice, within thirty (30) days of receipt of the invoice, subject to deduction of tax at source as required under applicable tax laws.]

5.3 **Vesting conditions.** Vesting under any Alternative is conditional on the Advisor continuing to provide the Services under this Agreement on each vesting date, and on the Advisor not being in material breach of this Agreement. Vesting ceases on the date of termination of this Agreement for any reason, save as expressly provided in Schedule 2 for acceleration on a Liquidity Event.

5.4 **Taxes.** The Advisor is responsible for all taxes payable by the Advisor on compensation received under this Agreement. The Company may deduct or withhold from any payment, or require the Advisor to pay before any issue of shares, any amount the Company is required to deduct or withhold under applicable tax laws. Where the Advisor is registered for GST and any fees are subject to GST, the Advisor shall issue a valid tax invoice.

5.5 **No other entitlement.** The Advisor acknowledges that no representation has been made to the Advisor as to the value of any shares, options or Phantom Units, which may have no value.

## 6. Intellectual Property

6.1 **Assignment.** The Advisor hereby assigns to the Company, absolutely, by way of present assignment of existing and future rights, all rights, title and interest in and to the Advisory Work Product and all Intellectual Property Rights in it, including, in respect of copyright, all rights comprised in copyright under section 14 of the Copyright Act, 1957 (including the rights to reproduce, issue copies, perform, communicate to the public, make adaptations and translations, and, for computer programs, to sell or give on commercial rental). This assignment is: (a) for the full term of protection of those rights, including all renewals and extensions; (b) for the whole world; and (c) made in consideration of the compensation set out in Clause 5, which the Advisor acknowledges as adequate consideration for the purposes of section 19(3) of the Copyright Act, 1957. In respect of any Advisory Work Product that comes into existence after the Effective Date, the assignment shall take effect automatically on its creation.

6.2 **No lapse.** The Parties agree that the rights assigned under Clause 6.1 shall not lapse or revert to the Advisor by reason of the Company not exercising them within any period, and that section 19(4) of the Copyright Act, 1957 shall not apply.

6.3 **Disclosure and further assurance.** The Advisor shall promptly disclose to the Company all Advisory Work Product that may be patentable or registrable. During and after the Term, the Advisor shall, at the Company's request and expense, sign all documents (including deeds of assignment in a form suitable for recordal with the Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks or the Registrar of Copyrights) and do all things reasonably required to perfect, register, maintain and enforce the Company's rights in the Advisory Work Product anywhere in the world.

6.4 **Moral rights.** To the extent permitted by Applicable Law, the Advisor waives, and irrevocably undertakes not to assert against the Company, its licensees or successors in title, any rights under section 57 of the Copyright Act, 1957 or any equivalent rights anywhere in the world in respect of the Advisory Work Product, including in respect of any adaptation, modification or use of it without attribution.

> **Drafting note:** The enforceability in India of an outright waiver of the author's special (moral) rights under s.57 of the Copyright Act, 1957 is not settled. Clause 6.4 therefore combines a waiver with an undertaking not to assert them, which is the conventional approach.

6.5 **Advisor's background IP.** Nothing in this Agreement assigns to the Company any Intellectual Property Rights owned by the Advisor before the Effective Date or developed independently of the Services and without use of the Company's Confidential Information (**"Background IP"**). If the Advisor incorporates any Background IP into Advisory Work Product, the Advisor grants the Company a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, transferable and sub-licensable licence to use, reproduce, modify and exploit that Background IP as part of, or in connection with, the Advisory Work Product.

6.6 **Excluded subject matter.** The Advisor's general knowledge, skills and experience, and ideas of general application in the Advisor's field that do not include the Company's Confidential Information, are not Advisory Work Product.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1 The Advisor shall, during the Term and at all times after it: (a) keep the Confidential Information strictly confidential; (b) use it only to provide the Services; (c) not disclose it to any person without the Company's prior written consent, except as required by Applicable Law (in which case the Advisor shall, where lawful, give the Company prompt prior notice); and (d) protect it with at least reasonable care.

7.2 The Advisor shall not input any Confidential Information into any AI Tool, or use it to train, fine-tune or evaluate any AI model, without the Company's prior written consent.

7.3 On termination of this Agreement or earlier on request, the Advisor shall return or securely delete all Confidential Information in the Advisor's possession or control and confirm this in writing, except copies retained in automatic back-ups that are not readily accessible, which remain subject to this Clause 7.

7.4 If the Company is or becomes listed, or the Advisor otherwise receives unpublished price sensitive information about any listed company through the Services, the Advisor shall comply with the SEBI (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations, 2015 and the Company's code of conduct under them.

7.5 The Advisor's obligations under this Clause 7 survive termination of this Agreement for {{confidentiality_period}}, save in respect of trade secrets and source code, for which they continue for so long as the information remains confidential.

## 8. Conflicts of Interest and Other Activities

8.1 The Advisor has disclosed to the Company in Schedule 3 all current employments, directorships, advisory roles, consultancy engagements and material investments that may be relevant to the Company's business (**"Disclosed Interests"**). The Advisor shall promptly notify the Company in writing of any new role or interest that could reasonably give rise to a conflict with the Advisor's duties under this Agreement.

8.2 During the Term, the Advisor shall not, without the Company's prior written consent, provide advisory, consultancy or other services to, or hold a material interest in, any business that competes directly with the Company's business as described in Recital A. This restriction does not apply to the Disclosed Interests or to holding up to two per cent of the listed securities of any company for investment purposes.

8.3 The Advisor represents and warrants that entering into and performing this Agreement does not and will not breach any agreement or obligation binding on the Advisor (including any employment contract, confidentiality agreement or invention assignment obligation owed to an employer, university or other third party), and that the Advisor will not disclose to the Company, or use in providing the Services, any confidential information or intellectual property belonging to any third party without authority.

8.4 If the Advisor is employed by, or holds an academic position with, any other organisation, the Advisor confirms that they have obtained any consent required from that organisation to enter into this Agreement and that no Intellectual Property Rights in the Advisory Work Product will vest in that organisation.

> **Drafting note:** Clause 8.2 restricts competing activity only during the Term. Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 makes post-termination restraints of trade void (save the sale-of-goodwill exception), so do not extend Clause 8.2 beyond the Term; rely on confidentiality instead. Advisors employed by universities, research institutes or large companies often have prior IP assignment obligations; ask for a copy of the relevant policy and, if needed, a written consent from the employer.

## 9. Non-Solicitation

9.1 During the Term and for twelve (12) months after it ends, the Advisor shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit or entice away from the Company any employee or consultant of the Company with whom the Advisor had material dealings during the last twelve (12) months of the Term, other than through general advertisements not targeted at the Company's personnel.

> **Drafting note:** Indian courts have reached differing views on whether post-term non-solicitation covenants are void under s.27 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872. Clause 9.1 is narrow and time-limited to improve its prospects of enforcement, but enforceability is not assured.

## 10. Independent Status

10.1 The Advisor is engaged as an independent advisor. Nothing in this Agreement creates a relationship of employer and employee, principal and agent, or partnership between the Parties. The Advisor is not entitled to any salary, wages, gratuity, provident fund, employees' state insurance, bonus, leave, or other employment benefit, whether under the Code on Wages, 2019, the Code on Social Security, 2020 or otherwise.

10.2 The Advisor shall determine the manner in which the Services are provided, subject to the Company's reasonable requirements as to scope and timing, and is responsible for the Advisor's own equipment, insurance and taxes.

10.3 If the Advisor is or becomes a director of the Company, the Advisor's duties as a director under the Companies Act, 2013 are in addition to, and not limited by, this Agreement.

> **Drafting note:** Labels do not decide status. Indian courts and authorities look at the substance of the relationship (control over how work is done, integration into the organisation, fixed hours, exclusivity, provision of tools, and economic dependence). An "advisor" who works fixed hours, manages staff or is paid a monthly fee resembling a salary may be treated as an employee under the Code on Wages, 2019, the Code on Social Security, 2020 and the applicable State Shops and Establishments Act, exposing the Company to provident fund, ESI and gratuity claims. Keep the engagement genuinely advisory, or issue an employment agreement instead (which would also make the person ESOP-eligible).

## 11. Term and Termination

11.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for {{advisor_term}} (the **"Term"**), unless terminated earlier under this Clause 11. The Parties may extend the Term by written agreement.

11.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, by giving the other not less than thirty (30) days' written notice.

11.3 The Company may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by written notice if the Advisor: (a) commits a material breach of this Agreement (including Clauses 6, 7 or 8) and, if the breach is capable of remedy, fails to remedy it within fifteen (15) days of being notified of it; (b) is convicted of any offence involving fraud, dishonesty or moral turpitude; (c) acts in a manner that brings, or is likely to bring, the Company into serious disrepute; or (d) is disqualified from acting as a director of a company under Applicable Law.

11.4 On termination or expiry: (a) vesting under Clause 5 and Schedule 2 ceases; (b) the treatment of vested compensation is as set out in Schedule 2 (and, for Alternative A, the ESOP Plan); (c) the Company shall reimburse expenses properly incurred before termination under Clause 4; and (d) the Advisor shall return all Company property and Confidential Information under Clause 7.3.

11.5 Clauses 1, 5 (to the extent of vested and accrued entitlements), 6, 7, 9, 11.4, 11.5 and 12 to 16 survive termination or expiry.

## 12. Limitation of Liability and Indemnity

12.1 The Company acknowledges that the Advisor provides advice only and that all decisions regarding the Company's business are taken by its Board and management, who remain responsible for them. Accordingly, except in respect of liability arising from a breach of Clause 6, 7 or 8, or from the Advisor's fraud, wilful misconduct or gross negligence, the Advisor's aggregate liability to the Company arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including negligence) or otherwise, shall not exceed the total cash amount (if any) paid to the Advisor under Clause 5 in the twelve (12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

12.2 Neither Party shall be liable to the other for any indirect or consequential loss, or for any loss of profit, revenue, business or goodwill, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, except in respect of a breach of Clause 6 or 7, or of fraud or wilful misconduct.

12.3 The Advisor shall indemnify the Company against all losses, liabilities, costs (including reasonable legal fees) and expenses arising out of any claim by a third party (including a current or former employer of the Advisor) that the Advisor's entry into or performance of this Agreement, or the Company's use of any Advisory Work Product in accordance with this Agreement, breaches any obligation owed by the Advisor to, or infringes any intellectual property right of, that third party.

12.4 Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes any liability that cannot be limited or excluded under Applicable Law.

> **Drafting note:** Indemnities are recognised under ss.124 and 125 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872. Individual advisors often resist any indemnity; the narrow indemnity in Clause 12.3 is limited to the most significant risk for the Company (third-party claims arising from the advisor's prior obligations), which the advisor controls. If the Advisor sits on the Board, consider whether the Company's directors' and officers' liability insurance should extend to them, and note that the Companies Act, 2013 restricts indemnification of directors against certain liabilities [verify].
